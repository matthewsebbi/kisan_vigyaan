# Niger Seed + Alternaria leaf spot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- stems_and_aerial_parts

### Symptom Class

- foliar_leaf_spot
- blight

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `circular_to_irregular_spot`
- Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
- Width profile: `localized`
- Ends: `irregular_or_defined`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

- small to expanding leaf spots; individual spots may enlarge and merge into larger blighted areas

### Color

- Center / primary color: brown_to_dark_brown
- Margin: dark_brown_to_black
- Surrounding tissue: green
- Color transition: `distinct_to_gradual`
- Current/overall colors: brown, dark_brown, black, green

### Texture

- Current texture: dry, necrotic, brittle_in_advanced_lesions
- Early appearance: `small_brown_spots`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `non_directional_on_leaf_blade`
- Distribution: `scattered_to_coalescing`
- Coalescence: `moderate_to_extensive_in_severe_cases`
- Vein relationship: `not_strictly_vein_limited`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `leaf_blade`
- Secondary locations: stems_and_other_aerial_parts

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- increasing_leaf_spotting
- blighting
- defoliation_in_severe_cases
- reduced_green_leaf_area
- possible_reduction_in_seed_set_and_yield

## Hallmark Features

- brown_to_black_leaf_spots
- concentric_rings_may_be_visible
- lesions_can_enlarge_and_coalesce
- severe_disease_can_progress_to_blight_and_defoliation
- primarily_foliar_symptoms

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- white_powdery_growth_as_dominant_symptom
- small_straw_to_brown_spots_with_gray_centers_as_the_dominant_pattern
- rust_pustules
- diffuse_whole_plant_yellowing_without_leaf_lesions
- water_soaked_angular_lesions_as_the_primary_pattern

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Niger Seed + Cercospora leaf spot
- Niger Seed + Powdery mildew
- Niger Seed + Rust

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare lesion color and geometry before interpreting overall leaf damage.
- Concentric rings support an Alternaria-type lesion pattern when clearly visible.
- Distinguish brown-to-black lesions from Cercospora spots that characteristically have straw-to-brown coloration with gray centers.
- Distinguish discrete necrotic lesions from diffuse powdery growth.
- Evaluate coalescence separately from individual lesion shape.
- Treat uncertainty in lesion margins or concentric-ring visibility as uncertainty rather than as a confirmed feature.
