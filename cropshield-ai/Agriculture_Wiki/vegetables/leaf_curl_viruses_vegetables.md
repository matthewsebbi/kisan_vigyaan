# Vegetables (Unspecified) - Leaf Curl Viruses

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- apical_shoot
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- leaf_deformation
- stunting
- chlorosis

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `none_no_distinct_lesion`
- Elongation: `not_applicable`
- Width profile: `not_applicable`
- Ends: `not_applicable`
- Discrete or continuous: `systemic_continuous`

### Size

- Affects entire leaves and shoots, leading to severely reduced overall leaf blade area.

### Color

- Center / primary color: not_applicable_no_distinct_lesion
- Margin: yellow_chlorotic
- Surrounding tissue: pale_green
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: yellow, pale_green, chlorotic

### Texture

- Current texture: thickened, leathery, brittle, puckered
- Early appearance: `upward_or_downward_rolling_of_margins`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `vertical_curling_and_cupping`
- Distribution: `systemic_apical_dominance`
- Coalescence: `not_applicable`
- Vein relationship: `vein_thickening_and_enation`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `apical_meristem_and_young_terminal_leaves`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- severe_stunting
- bushy_stunted_growth_habit
- flower_abortion
- drastic_reduction_in_fruit_yield
- whitefly_vector_presence

## Hallmark Features

- pronounced_upward_or_downward_leaf_margin_curling
- severe_stunting_and_shortened_internodes
- thickened_brittle_leathery_leaf_texture
- chlorosis_along_leaf_margins_and_interveinal_areas
- vein_thickening_or_enations_on_underside_of_leaves

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- water_soaked_angular_spots
- powdery_spore_masses
- necrotic_target_rings
- severe_leaf_chewing_or_holes

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Broad Mite / Spider Mite Damage
- Herbicide Hormone Damage (2,4-D drift)
- Drought / Moisture Stress Leaf Rolling

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Distinguish thick, brittle curled leaves with vein enations from floppy leaf rolling caused by moisture stress.
- Check for vector presence (whiteflies) vs. mite infestations on leaf undersides.
- Observe systemic apical stunting and flower drop which differentiates viral infection from localized mechanical insect damage.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 28°C – 38°C (High temperatures accelerate whitefly vector population growth and viral development)

### Humidity

- Low to moderate relative humidity (favors vector flight activity)

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Dry conditions with low rainfall encourage high vector pressure

### Soil

- Not specified in supplied file.

## Crop Stage

- Early seedling stage through active vegetative stage (early infection leads to complete crop loss)
