# Vegetables (Unspecified) - Black Rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- petiole
- stem
- vascular_system

### Symptom Class

- V_shaped_marginal_chlorosis_and_necrosis
- vascular_blackening
- necrotic_blight

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `v_shaped_wedge`
- Elongation: `moderate_extending_inward_along_veins`
- Width profile: `broad_at_margin_narrowing_towards_midrib`
- Ends: `pointed_towards_vein_or_midrib`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_becoming_coalescent`

### Size

- Begins as small chlorotic marginal wedges (0.5–1 cm) expanding inward along major leaf veins to cover wide sections of the leaf lamina (5–15+ cm).

### Color

- Center / primary color: dull_yellow_to_brown_necrotic
- Margin: bright_yellow_chlorotic
- Surrounding tissue: pale_green_to_dark_blackened_veins
- Color transition: `gradual_to_abrupt`
- Current/overall colors: dull_yellow, brown, black, bright_yellow

### Texture

- Current texture: papery, dry, brittle_in_advanced_stage, wilted
- Early appearance: `marginal_yellowing_with_blackened_vein_network`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `wedge_shaped_pointing_inward`
- Distribution: `marginal_hydathode_originated`
- Coalescence: `moderate_to_high`
- Vein relationship: `blackened_veins_within_and_ahead_of_chlorotic_zone`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `leaf_margins_hydathodes_extending_towards_midrib`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- blackening_of_internal_vascular_ring
- lower_leaf_drop_defoliation
- uncoordinated_stunting
- head_rot_or_curd_rot
- systemic_wilting

## Hallmark Features

- characteristic_v_shaped_yellow_to_brown_wedge_lesions_at_leaf_margins
- distinct_blackening_of_leaf_veins_and_vascular_tissue
- infection_originates_from_hydathodes_or_marginal_wounds
- dark_blackened_ring_visible_in_stem_cross_section
- lack_of_foul_soft_rot_odor_unless_secondary_invaders_are_present

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- powdery_white_fungal_growth
- concentric_ring_target_spots
- water_soaked_angular_spots_bounded_only_by_minor_veins
- severe_upward_leaf_curling_without_necrosis

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Xanthomonas Leaf Spot
- Alternaria Leaf Spot / Blight
- Nitrogen or Potassium Deficiency (marginal scorch)
- Fusarium / Verticillium Wilt

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Look for distinct V-shaped marginal chlorosis/necrosis with blackened vein networks inside the lesion to differentiate from nutrient burn.
- Cut stems or petioles to observe internal blackening of the vascular bundle ring.
- Distinguish the characteristic marginal wedge shape originating at hydathodes from scattered fungal target spots.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 25°C – 30°C (Warm conditions strongly promote bacterial proliferation and systemic movement)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (> 80–90%) and prolonged dew or fog periods

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Heavy splash rainfall, overhead irrigation, wet foliage favoring hydathode entry

### Soil

- Infested crop residue or soil acting as primary inoculum source; high nitrogen levels increasing susceptibility

## Crop Stage

- Vegetative through crop maturity / head formation
