### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `none_no_distinct_lesion`
- Elongation: `not_applicable`

- Ends: `not_applicable`

### Size

- Affects systemic entire leaf lamina across multiple leaves and foliage layers.

### Color

- Center / primary color: not_applicable_no_distinct_lesion
- Margin: not_applicable
- Surrounding tissue: light_green_to_yellow
- Color transition: `gradual_to_variegated`
- Current/overall colors: dark_green, light_green, yellow, chlorotic

### Texture

- Current texture: blistered, puckered, rough_leathery, uneven_surface
- Early appearance: `interveinal_clearing`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `mottled_mosaic_patchwork`
- Distribution: `systemic_widespread`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `vein_clearing_or_vein_banding`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `young_expanding_leaves_and_apical_growth`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- stunting
- leaf_shoe_stringing_or_narrowing
- malformed_distorted_fruit
- reduced_fruit_set
- yield_loss

## Hallmark Features

- alternating_dark_green_and_light_green_yellow_patches
- leaf_puckering_and_blistering
- vein_clearing_or_vein_banding
- systemic_expression_on_younger_leaves
- lack_of_primary_necrotic_tissue_in_early_stages

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- water_soaked_necrotic_lesions
- powdery_white_fungal_mats
- sharp_concentric_necrotic_rings
- bacterial_ooze

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Nutrient Deficiencies (e.g., Nitrogen, Magnesium, Iron chlorosis)
- Herbicide Drift Injury (e.g., glyphosate/2,4-D damage)
- Aphid / Thrips Feeding Distortion

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Distinguish true variegated mosaic (dark/light green islands) from uniform interveinal chlorosis of nutrient deficiencies.
- Verify absence of leaf tissue necrosis in early-to-mid infection stages.
- Check systemic presence on new growth rather than uniform spray-drift pattern across older exposed leaves.
