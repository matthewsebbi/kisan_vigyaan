# Vegetables (Unspecified) - Bacterial Blight

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- petiole
- stem
- fruit

### Symptom Class

- water_soaked_lesions
- necrotic_blight
- chlorotic_halo

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_angular`
- Elongation: `moderate_to_high`
- Width profile: `variable`
- Ends: `angular_restricted_by_veins`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_becoming_coalescent_continuous`

### Size

- Small water-soaked spots (1–3 mm) expanding and coalescing into large irregular blighted areas covering multi-cm spans of leaf surface.

### Color

- Center / primary color: water_soaked_gray_green_to_dark_brown_black
- Margin: dark_brown_to_black_necrotic
- Surrounding tissue: yellow_chlorotic_halo
- Color transition: `abrupt`
- Current/overall colors: dark_brown, tan, black, bright_yellow

### Texture

- Current texture: papery, brittle, dry_in_advanced_stage, slimy_ooze_under_high_humidity
- Early appearance: `water_soaked_translucent`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `random_to_vein_delimited`
- Distribution: `scattered_becoming_extensive_blight`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `restricted_by_minor_veins_in_early_stage`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `lower_to_mid_canopy_leaf_blades_and_margins`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- defoliation
- stem_cankers
- bacterial_ooze_exudate
- plant_wilting
- sunscald_on_exposed_fruit

## Hallmark Features

- water_soaked_angular_lesions
- yellow_chlorotic_halo_surrounding_necrotic_center
- vein_bounded_lesion_margins
- presence_of_bacterial_ooze_in_humid_conditions
- rapid_coalescence_leading_to_leaf_blight_and_defoliation

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- concentric_rings_target_pattern
- powdery_white_spore_growth
- distinct_mosaic_mottling
- upward_leaf_curling_without_necrosis

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Alternaria Leaf Spot / Early Blight
- Powdery Mildew
- Xanthomonas / Pseudomonas Leaf Spot variants

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare affected plant parts before interpreting lesion color.
- Distinguish bacterial water-soaked angular margins bounded by veins from fungal concentric target rings.
- Check for bacterial exudate or droplets on the underside of young lesions during high humidity.
- Distinguish rapid tissue collapse and necrosis from viral chlorosis or curling.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 25°C – 32°C (Warm conditions favor rapid bacterial multiplication)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (> 85%)

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Heavy rainfall, overhead irrigation, dew formation, driving wind-rain splash

### Soil

- Poorly drained soils promoting high canopy humidity; wet soil splash onto low foliage

## Crop Stage

- Vegetative through fruiting stages (susceptible at all growth stages, especially post-flowering)
