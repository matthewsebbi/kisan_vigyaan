# Guava Anthracnose

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- fruit
- petiole
- stem

### Symptom Class

- sunken_necrotic_lesions
- fruit_rot
- dieback

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `circular_to_subcircular_sunken`
- Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
- Width profile: `concentric_expanding`
- Ends: `rounded_to_irregular`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_becoming_coalescent`

### Size

- Leaf spots range from 2–8 mm; fruit lesions begin as pinhead spots and expand to large sunken necrotic cavities covering 2–5+ cm or entire fruits.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown_to_black
- Margin: dark_reddish_brown_border
- Surrounding tissue: chlorotic_yellow_halo
- Color transition: `abrupt`
- Current/overall colors: dark_brown, black, salmon_pink_spore_masses, yellow

### Texture

- Current texture: sunken, dry, hard_coriaceous, slimy_pink_exudate_under_humid_conditions
- Early appearance: `pinhead_dark_pinpoint_spots`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `concentric_expanding_outward`
- Distribution: `scattered_on_leaves_and_fruits`
- Coalescence: `moderate_to_high_on_ripe_fruit`
- Vein relationship: `crosses_veins_unrestricted`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `developing_and_mature_fruits_young_foliage_and_twigs`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- dieback_of_terminal_twigs
- mummification_of_unripe_and_ripe_fruits
- premature_leaf_and_fruit_drop
- acervuli_producing_salmon_pink_spore_masses

## Hallmark Features

- circular_sunken_dark_brown_to_black_lesions_on_fruits
- presence_of_salmon_pink_or_rusty_orange_spore_masses_in_lesion_center
- twig_dieback_progressing_downward
- leaf_spots_with_dark_brown_margins_and_yellow_halos
- fruit_mummification_remaining_attached_to_tree

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- internal_vascular_browning_without_fruit_symptoms
- upward_leaf_rolling_with_thickened_enations
- powdery_white_mycelial_mats
- root_decay_or_bark_sloughing

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Guava Wilt
- Pestalotiopsis Leaf and Fruit Spot
- Phytophthora Fruit Rot

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Presence of distinct sunken black fruit lesions with pinkish spore drops discriminates Anthracnose from Guava Wilt.
- Examine twig dieback for acervuli rather than vascular stem browning.
- Anthracnose lesions are firm and sunken with spore masses, unlike soft, water-soaked, foul-smelling bacterial rots.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Warm conditions ranging from 25°C to 30°C promote optimal fungal germination and infection.

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (> 85%) is required for spore production and acervuli formation.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Frequent rainfall, high canopy moisture, and wind-blown splash disperse *Colletotrichum* conidia.

### Soil

- Poor drainage and dense planting can create favorable humid microclimates within the canopy.

## Crop Stage

- Flowering through fruit development and post-harvest storage stage.
