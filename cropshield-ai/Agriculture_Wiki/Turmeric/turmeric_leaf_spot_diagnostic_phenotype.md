# Turmeric Leaf Spot (Colletotrichum capsici)

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade

### Symptom Class

- discrete_foliar_leaf_spot
- necrotic_spot_with_halo

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `elliptic_to_oval`
- Elongation: `moderate_along_vein_axis`
- Width profile: `centered_necrotic_spot`
- Ends: `tapered_to_rounded`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_becoming_coalescent_in_severe_cases`

### Size

- Typically ranges from 1 cm to 5 cm in length and 0.5 cm to 2 cm in width.

### Color

- Center / primary color: grey_to_pale_brown
- Margin: dark_brown_to_reddish_brown
- Surrounding tissue: yellow_chlorotic_halo
- Color transition: `abrupt`
- Current/overall colors: grey, pale_brown, dark_brown, chlorotic_yellow

### Texture

- Current texture: dry, papery, thin, brittle_in_center, concentrated_black_acervuli_dots
- Early appearance: `small_yellowish_brown_spot`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `parallel_to_leaf_veins`
- Distribution: `scattered_across_leaf_surface`
- Coalescence: `moderate_forming_large_necrotic_patches`
- Vein relationship: `slightly_bounded_by_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `upper_and_lower_surface_of_leaf_blade`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- premature_drying_and_shriveling_of_leaves
- burnt_appearance_of_foliage_in_severe_outbreaks
- reduced_photosynthetic_area_and_yield_loss
- central_leaf_shot_holes_when_dry_centers_fall_out

## Hallmark Features

- elliptic_or_oval_spots_with_grey_centers_and_dark_brown_margins
- chlorotic_yellow_halo_surrounding_individual_lesions
- presence_of_concentric_black_dots_acervuli_in_grey_center
- papery_brittle_center_that_may_drop_out

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- soft_water_soaked_foul_smelling_rhizome_decay
- small_yellow_rectangular_blotches_arranged_in_rows
- diffuse_yellow_orange_discoloration_without_spots
- white_downy_mold_underneath_leaves

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Leaf Blotch (*Taphrina maculans*)
- Curvularia Leaf Spot
- Herbicide / Chemical Spray Burn

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Examine spot shape and center: Colletotrichum spots are large, elliptic, and have dry grey centers with distinct black fruiting bodies (acervuli).
- Taphrina Leaf Blotch produces much smaller, reddish-brown rectangular/golden-yellow blotches in rows rather than large elliptic grey spots.
- Check rhizome health: Foliar spots do not cause pseudostem collar soft rot or foul odor.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Favored by temperatures between 23°C and 28°C.

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (>80%) required for spore germination and infection.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Frequent rainfall, high canopy wetness, and continuous overcast conditions promote disease spread.

### Soil

- Nutrient deficiency or unamended soils increase host susceptibility.

## Crop Stage

- Appears during the active vegetative growth phase (August to November).
