# Turmeric Rhizome Rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- rhizome
- pseudostem
- roots
- leaf_blade
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- soft_rot_decay
- pseudostem_collar_rot
- foliar_yellowing_and_wilting

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_soft_water_soaked_decay`
- Elongation: `variable_extending_along_pseudostem_base`
- Width profile: `girdling_collar_and_rhizome_core`
- Ends: `diffuse_decay_margins`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_expanding_rot`

### Size

- Affects entire underground rhizome system and pseudostem base; ranges from localized internal soft rot lesions to full collapse of the clump.

### Color

- Center / primary color: soft_water_soaked_brown_to_dark_brown
- Margin: dark_water_soaked_brown
- Surrounding tissue: pale_yellow_to_chlorotic
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: water_soaked_brown, dark_brown, chlorotic_yellow, brown_necrotic

### Texture

- Current texture: soft, watery, mushy, decaying, foul_smelling, brittle_pseudostem_base
- Early appearance: `water_soaked_soft_lesion_at_collar_region`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `basipetal_and_acropetal_decay`
- Distribution: `systemic_clump_decay`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `rhizome_and_pseudostem_collar_base_spreading_to_leaves`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- yellowing_of_lower_leaves_moving_upward
- drooping_and_wilting_of_foliage
- easy_pulling_off_of_affected_tillers_from_collar
- rotting_and_foul_smelling_underground_rhizomes
- patch_wise_drying_and_collapse_in_field

## Hallmark Features

- water_soaked_soft_rot_at_pseudostem_base_and_rhizome
- easy_detachment_of_tillers_with_a_gentle_pull
- foul_decaying_odor_from_rhizome_and_collar
- progressive_foliar_yellowing_and_wilting_from_bottom_up

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- discrete_elliptic_brown_leaf_spots_with_grey_centers
- reddish_brown_concentric_blotch_bands_on_leaves
- superficial_black_crusts_without_internal_rot
- powdery_white_sporulation_on_foliage

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Bacterial Wilt (*Ralstonia solanacearum*)
- Dry Rot (*Fusarium oxysporum*)
- Waterlogging / Root Asphyxiation

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Gently pull the base of the yellowing tiller: Rhizome rot allows easy detachment at the soft, decayed collar with a foul smell.
- Inspect internal rhizome tissues: Soft rot turns internal flesh watery and soft, unlike the dry vascular discoloration of Fusarium dry rot.
- Differentiate from leaf spot/blotch diseases where leaf blade lesions occur without basal soft rot.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- High soil temperatures ranging from 25°C to 35°C accelerate pathogen proliferation.

### Humidity

- High relative atmospheric humidity combined with waterlogged soil conditions.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Heavy monsoon rains, water stagnation, flooding, and poor field drainage trigger severe outbreaks.

### Soil

- Heavy clay soils with poor internal drainage favor spore germination and movement.

## Crop Stage

- Most critical during rhizome initiation, development, and maturity stages (July to October in typical monsoon cycles).
