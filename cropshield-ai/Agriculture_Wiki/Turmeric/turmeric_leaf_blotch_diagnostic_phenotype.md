# Turmeric Leaf Blotch (Taphrina maculans)

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade

### Symptom Class

- superficial_blotch
- small_numerous_chlorotic_spots

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `small_rectangular_to_oval_spots`
- Elongation: `low_to_moderate_following_veins`
- Width profile: `narrow_interveinal_blotch`
- Ends: `blunt_to_rounded`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_in_dense_parallel_rows`

### Size

- Individual spots are small, typically 1 mm to 2 mm wide and up to 3 mm long; coalesce to cover large leaf areas.

### Color

- Center / primary color: reddish_brown_to_dirty_yellow
- Margin: golden_yellow_to_orange
- Surrounding tissue: green_to_yellowish
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: golden_yellow, reddish_brown, orange, dark_brown

### Texture

- Current texture: slightly_raised_rough_leaf_surface, dry, leathery
- Early appearance: `small_pale_yellow_flecks`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `arranged_in_rows_parallel_to_veins`
- Distribution: `densely_clustered_on_both_leaf_surfaces`
- Coalescence: `high_forming_large_brown_blotches`
- Vein relationship: `strictly_interveinal`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `both_upper_and_lower_surfaces_of_leaves`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- golden_yellow_to_reddish_brown_sheet_like_appearance_of_canopy
- premature_leaf_drying
- severe_photosynthetic_reduction
- reduced_rhizome_yield

## Hallmark Features

- small_numerous_golden_yellow_to_reddish_brown_spots
- arrangement_of_spots_in_dense_rows_parallel_to_leaf_veins
- involvement_of_both_upper_and_lower_leaf_surfaces
- coalescing_spots_giving_a_yellowish_brown_blotch_appearance

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- large_elliptic_spots_with_grey_centers_and_black_acervuli
- soft_rotting_water_soaked_rhizomes
- systemic_leaf_cupping_and_stunting_without_spots
- dark_black_crusty_sclerotia

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Leaf Spot (*Colletotrichum capsici*)
- Phaeodactylium Leaf Spot
- Physiological Sunscald

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Observe spot size and density: Taphrina leaf blotch consists of hundreds of tiny, crowded, golden-yellow/reddish-brown spots in rows parallel to veins.
- Distinguish from Colletotrichum leaf spot which forms fewer, much larger, isolated elliptic spots with grey centers.
- Confirm presence of spots on both leaf surfaces with a rough, slightly raised surface texture.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Moderate temperatures around 20°C to 25°C favor spore germinating and infection.

### Humidity

- High atmospheric humidity (>85%) required.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Dew drops, mist, and light rain showers facilitate ascospore dispersal and leaf infection.

### Soil

- Poor soil drainage and overcrowded shading worsen disease intensity.

## Crop Stage

- Appears in early-to-mid growth stages (July to October).
