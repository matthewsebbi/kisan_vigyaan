# Cotton Black Arm / Bacterial Blight

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- stem_and_twigs
- boll
- cotyledon

### Symptom Class

- angular_water_soaked_lesion
- black_stem_canker
- circular_water_soaked_boll_spot

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `angular_vein_delimited_spots_on_leaves_and_elongated_black_cankers_on_stems`
- Elongation: `high_on_stems_and_petioles_low_on_leaves`
- Width profile: `angular_restricted_by_veins`
- Ends: `angular_or_pointed`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

- Angular leaf spots: 1 mm to 5 mm
- Black arm stem lesions: several centimeters long, girdling branches

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown_to_black_with_whitish_bacterial_exudate_crust
- Margin: water_soaked_dark_green_turning_dark_purple
- Surrounding tissue: green_to_yellowish
- Color transition: `sharp`
- Current/overall colors: water_soaked_green, dark_brown, black, purple

### Texture

- Current texture: dry, brittle, greasy, glazed_with_dried_bacterial_ooze
- Early appearance: `water_soaked_greasy_angular_droplets_underneath_leaf`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `vein_bound_on_leaves_longitudinal_on_stems`
- Distribution: `foliar_canopy_stems_and_bolls`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `strictly_vein_bound`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `lower_leaf_surface_cotyledons_branches_and_boll_carpel_walls`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- angular_water_soaked_leaf_spots_turning_necrotic
- dark_brown_to_black_cankers_girdling_stems_and_petioles_black_arm
- premature_leaf_drop_defoliation
- stem_breakage_at_infected_nodes
- sunken_water_soaked_round_spots_on_bolls_leading_to_internal_rot

## Hallmark Features

- angular_vein_restricted_water_soaked_lesions_on_underside_of_leaves
- dark_black_elongated_cankers_girdling_branches_black_arm_phase
- dried_whitish_amber_bacterial_ooze_crusts_on_lesions

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- salmon_pink_spore_masses_on_bolls
- thickened_leaf_veins_with_cup_shaped_enations
- bark_shredding_at_root_level
- vascular_xylem_ring_staining_without_external_cankers

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Alternaria Leaf Spot
- Cercospora Leaf Spot
- Cotton Anthracnose

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Bacterial Blight leaf lesions are strictly angular and vein-bound with water-soaked greasy margins, unlike fungal spots which are circular and non-vein-restricted.
- Presence of bacterial ooze crusts upon drying distinguishes it from fungal blights.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 30°C - 35°C (Warm to hot conditions)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (>80%)

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Rainstorms, overhead sprinkler irrigation, and wind-blown rain propel bacterial splash dispersion.

### Soil

- Poorly drained or continuously wet soils favour disease survival in debris.

## Crop Stage

- All crop stages (Cotyledonary, vegetative, flowering, and boll formation)
