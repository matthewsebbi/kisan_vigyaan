# Cotton Root Rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- tap_root
- lateral_roots
- crown_collar_region
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- root_rot
- sudden_wilt
- bark_shredding_and_decay

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_decay_and_bark_shredding_around_taproot`
- Elongation: `high_along_root_axis`
- Width profile: `encircling_root`
- Ends: `irregular`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_decay`

### Size

- Involves total taproot system and root collar; field circular dying spots expand to several meters wide.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown_to_black_rotten_bark_with_yellowish_dirty_white_mycelial_mats
- Margin: dark_brown
- Surrounding tissue: yellow_to_bronze_withered_foliage
- Color transition: `distinct`
- Current/overall colors: dark_brown, black, yellowish_white, bronze, brown

### Texture

- Current texture: soft, spongy, wet_or_dry_shredded_bark, easily_peeled
- Early appearance: `sudden_bronzing_and_flagging_of_upper_leaves`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `encircling_root_system`
- Distribution: `root_zone_expanding_in_circular_field_patches`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `taproot_below_soil_line_and_root_collar`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- sudden_and_rapid_wilting_of_entire_plant_within_24_to_48_hours
- dead_leaves_remain_attached_to_withered_stems
- bark_of_taproot_shredded_rotted_and_peels_off_easily
- yellowish_dirty_white_fungal_mats_on_soil_surface_around_dead_plants
- circular_spreading_patches_of_dead_plants_in_field

## Hallmark Features

- rapid_sudden_wilting_with_dead_leaves_clinging_to_the_plant
- taproot_bark_is_shredded_brown_and_sloughs_off_easily
- presence_of_yellowish_white_fungal_strands_or_mats_at_soil_line

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- internal_vascular_ring_staining_with_intact_external_root_bark
- angular_vein_bound_foliar_spots
- leaf_enations_and_thickened_veins
- pink_slimy_spore_masses_on_bolls

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Fusarium Wilt
- Verticillium Wilt
- Lightning Strike

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Root Rot causes complete external decay and easily peelable/shredded bark on taproots, unlike Vascular Wilts where the root bark remains intact and only internal xylem is discolored.
- Leaves remain attached to the plant after rapid wilt in Cotton Root Rot, whereas Vascular Wilts often cause defoliation.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 28°C - 38°C (Hot summer conditions favor rapid wilting)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity following soil wetting.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Heavy rains or irrigation following hot dry weather trigger intense disease outbreaks.

### Soil

- Heavy, alkaline, calcareous soils with poor aeration favor Phymatotrichopsis; wet poorly drained soils favor Rhizoctonia/Macrophomina.

## Crop Stage

- Flowering to boll development stage (peak warm summer months)
