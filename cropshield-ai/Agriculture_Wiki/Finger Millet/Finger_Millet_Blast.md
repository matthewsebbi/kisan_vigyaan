# Finger Millet Blast

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- necrotic_leaf_lesion
- leaf_blast

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `spindle_diamond`
- Elongation: `high`
- Width profile: `wider_at_center`
- Ends: `pointed_tapered`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

- Small brown lesions may enlarge into elongated spindle-shaped lesions.
- Several lesions may merge into large necrotic areas and cause drying of foliage.

### Color

- Center / primary color: `gray_to_grayish`
- Margin: `brown_to_dark_brown`
- Surrounding tissue: green_to_yellow_chlorotic
- Color transition: `sharp_to_gradual`
- Current/overall colors: brown, gray, grayish_brown, dark_brown, yellow_chlorotic

### Texture

- Current texture: dry, necrotic, papery_in_advanced_lesions
- Early appearance: `brown_water_soaked_or_unclear`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `longitudinal_to_diagonal`
- Distribution: `discrete_scattered`
- Coalescence: `may_coalesce`
- Vein relationship: `crosses_or_ignores_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `leaf_blade`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- multiple_leaf_lesions
- foliage_drying
- reduced_green_leaf_area
- premature_leaf_death_in_severe_cases
- disease_can_occur_from_seedling_to_grain_formation_stage

## Hallmark Features

- spindle_shaped_leaf_spots
- brown_margin
- necrotic_gray_center
- several_spots_can_coalesce

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- blackened_shrunken_neck_as_the_primary_feature
- blackened_fingers_with_poor_seed_setting_as_the_primary_feature
- raised_powdery_reddish_brown_pustules
- diffuse_yellow_orange_discoloration_without_distinct_lesions
- honeydew_exudation_from_florets
- smut_spore_balls

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Finger Millet Neck Blast
- Finger Millet Finger Blast
- Finger Millet Brown Spot / Leaf Spot
- Finger Millet Rust

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Identify the affected plant part before interpreting lesion color.
- Give high diagnostic weight to spindle-shaped lesions with brown margins and gray necrotic centers.
- Distinguish leaf blast from neck blast by confirming that the main lesion is on the leaf rather than the neck.
- Distinguish leaf blast from finger blast by checking whether the earhead fingers are primarily affected.
- Distinguish flat necrotic lesions from raised rust pustules.
- Treat uncertainty in lesion boundaries and plant-part identification as uncertainty rather than as a fact.
