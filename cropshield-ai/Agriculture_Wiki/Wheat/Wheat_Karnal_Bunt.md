## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   entire_spike_replaced_by_black_spore_mass
-   yellow_pustules_in_longitudinal_leaf_rows
-   orange_brown_leaf_pustules
-   elongated_black_stem_pustules

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Wheat Loose Smut
-   Wheat Stripe / Yellow Rust
-   Wheat Leaf / Brown Rust
-   Wheat Stem / Black Rust
-   Grain mold

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare the level of affected tissue: individual grain versus whole
    spike or spikelet.
-   Distinguish partial kernel discoloration from complete
    reproductive-structure replacement.
-   Compare external powdery spore material with internal or localized
    grain symptoms.
-   Do not infer diagnosis from black color alone because several grain
    or smut problems can appear dark.
-   Treat uncertainty in grain involvement or symptom extent as
    uncertainty rather than as a fact.
