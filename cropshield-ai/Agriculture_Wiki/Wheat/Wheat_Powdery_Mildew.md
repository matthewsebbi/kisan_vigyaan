# Wheat Powdery Mildew

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   leaf_sheath
-   stem
-   spike
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   superficial_fungal_growth
-   diffuse_discoloration
-   chlorotic_lesions

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `irregular_superficial_patches`
-   Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
-   Width profile: `variable`
-   Ends: `irregular`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small white fungal patches may enlarge into irregular areas covering
    substantial portions of leaves or other green plant surfaces

### Color

-   Center / primary color: white, gray_white
-   Margin: pale_green, yellow_green, not_distinct
-   Surrounding tissue: green
-   Color transition: `gradual`
-   Current/overall colors: white, gray_white, pale_gray, yellow_green,
    brown_in_older_tissue

### Texture

-   Current texture: powdery, superficial, slightly_rough
-   Early appearance: `small_white_powdery_patches`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: irregular
-   Distribution: discrete_patches_that_may_coalesce
-   Coalescence: moderate_to_extensive
-   Vein relationship: crosses_or_ignores_veins

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location: leaf_surface

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   white_powdery_growth_on_leaf_surface
-   irregular_expanding_patches
-   chlorotic_or_yellowed_tissue_under_heavy_growth
-   progressive_loss_of_green_leaf_area
-   severe_affected_tissue_may_brown_or_dry

## Hallmark Features

-   white_to_gray_white_powdery_surface_growth
-   superficial_fungal_colonies
-   irregular_patches
-   patches_can_coalesce
-   green_leaf_surface_contrasts_with_white_growth

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   raised_orange_brown_rust_pustules
-   bright_yellow_pustules_in_linear_rows
-   black_stem_pustules
-   discrete_dark_brown_necrotic_spots_without_surface_growth
-   black_spore_mass_replacing_spikelets

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Wheat Stripe / Yellow Rust
-   Wheat Leaf / Brown Rust
-   Wheat Stem / Black Rust
-   Septoria Leaf Blotch
-   Sooty mold or other superficial fungal growth

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare surface texture and elevation before interpreting color.
-   Distinguish white powdery superficial growth from raised rust
    pustules.
-   Compare patch geometry and distribution with discrete rust pustules
    or necrotic leaf spots.
-   Check whether the white growth is superficial and powder-like rather
    than a colored spore mass.
-   Treat uncertainty in surface growth or lesion texture as uncertainty
    rather than as a fact.
