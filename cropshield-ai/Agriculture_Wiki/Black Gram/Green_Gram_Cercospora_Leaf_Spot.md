# Green Gram Cercospora Leaf Spot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   leaf_sheath
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   discrete_leaf_lesions
-   necrotic_spots

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `circular_to_angular_lesions`
-   Elongation: `low`
-   Width profile: `wider_at_center`
-   Ends: `sharp_angular_or_rounded`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small to medium discrete leaf spots that may enlarge and merge,
    producing larger necrotic areas

### Color

-   Center / primary color: gray, gray_brown, pale_brown
-   Margin: reddish_brown, dark_brown, purple_brown
-   Surrounding tissue: green
-   Color transition: `sharp_two_tone`
-   Current/overall colors: gray, gray_brown, pale_brown, reddish_brown,
    dark_brown, purple_brown

### Texture

-   Current texture: dry_papery, slightly_rough, brittle_in_severe_stage
-   Early appearance: `small_pale_to_brown_spots`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: irregular
-   Distribution: discrete_scattered
-   Coalescence: moderate
-   Vein relationship: crosses_or_ignores_veins

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location: leaf_blade

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   scattered_leaf_spots
-   increasing_lesion_density
-   lesion_coalescence
-   progressive_leaf_necrosis
-   reduced_green_leaf_area

## Hallmark Features

-   discrete_circular_to_angular_leaf_spots
-   gray_to_brown_centers
-   darker_reddish_brown_or_purple_brown_margins
-   scattered_distribution
-   coalescing_lesions_in_severe_infection

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   bright_yellow_and_green_mosaic_without_necrotic_spots
-   large_irregular_dark_lesions_without_discrete_boundaries
-   white_powdery_surface_growth
-   raised_orange_brown_pustules
-   continuous_uniform_leaf_yellowing

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Green Gram Yellow Mosaic Disease
-   Green Gram Anthracnose
-   bacterial_leaf_spot
-   other fungal leaf spots

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare discrete lesion geometry before interpreting overall leaf
    discoloration.
-   Distinguish gray-brown necrotic spots from yellow-green mosaic
    patches.
-   Compare center-to-margin color relationships rather than color words
    in isolation.
-   Distinguish flat necrotic lesions from raised pustules or
    superficial fungal growth.
-   Treat uncertainty in lesion boundaries or vein relationship as
    uncertainty, not as a fact.
