# Green Gram Bacterial Leaf Blight

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   leaf_sheath
-   petiole
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   water_soaked_lesions
-   necrotic_lesions
-   leaf_blight

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `angular_to_irregular_lesions`
-   Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
-   Width profile: `variable`
-   Ends: `irregular_to_angular`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small water-soaked spots may enlarge into irregular angular lesions
    and merge into larger blighted areas

### Color

-   Center / primary color: water_soaked, pale_green, yellow_brown,
    brown
-   Margin: yellow, brown, reddish_brown
-   Surrounding tissue: green
-   Color transition: `gradual_to_sharp`
-   Current/overall colors: water_soaked, pale_green, yellow,
    yellow_brown, brown, reddish_brown

### Texture

-   Current texture: water_soaked_early, moist_to_dry_later, necrotic,
    brittle_in_severe_stage
-   Early appearance: `small_water_soaked_spots`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: irregular
-   Distribution: discrete_scattered_to_coalescing
-   Coalescence: moderate_to_extensive
-   Vein relationship: often_limited_by_veins_or_angular

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location: leaf_blade

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   water_soaked_leaf_spots
-   angular_or_irregular_lesions
-   progressive_leaf_blight
-   lesion_coalescence
-   extensive_leaf_necrosis_in_severe_cases

## Hallmark Features

-   early_water_soaked_leaf_spots
-   angular_to_irregular_lesions
-   yellow_to_brown_necrotic_tissue
-   lesion_coalescence
-   leaf_blight_with_loss_of_green_area

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   white_powdery_surface_growth
-   bright_yellow_and_green_mosaic_without_necrotic_lesions
-   raised_orange_brown_pustules
-   black_smut_material
-   web_like_fungal_growth_covering_lesions

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Green Gram Web Blight
-   Green Gram Cercospora Leaf Spot
-   Green Gram Anthracnose
-   Green Gram Powdery Mildew

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare early water-soaked appearance before interpreting later
    brown necrosis.
-   Distinguish angular or vein-limited lesions from irregular fungal
    blight lesions.
-   Compare surface texture to separate bacterial lesions from powdery
    or web-like fungal growth.
-   Distinguish flat water-soaked/necrotic lesions from raised pustules.
-   Treat uncertainty in lesion boundaries, water-soaked appearance, or
    vein relationship as uncertainty, not as a fact.
