# Green Gram Anthracnose

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   stem
-   petiole
-   pod
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   discrete_lesions
-   necrotic_spots

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `circular_to_oval_lesions`
-   Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
-   Width profile: `wider_at_center`
-   Ends: `blunt_rounded`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small to medium circular or oval lesions that may enlarge and merge;
    lesions can occur on leaves and other above-ground plant parts

### Color

-   Center / primary color: tan, brown, gray_brown
-   Margin: dark_brown, reddish_brown, blackish_brown
-   Surrounding tissue: green
-   Color transition: `sharp_two_tone`
-   Current/overall colors: tan, brown, gray_brown, dark_brown,
    reddish_brown, blackish_brown

### Texture

-   Current texture: dry_papery, slightly_rough, necrotic,
    brittle_in_severe_stage
-   Early appearance: `small_pale_to_brown_spots`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: irregular
-   Distribution: discrete_scattered
-   Coalescence: moderate_to_extensive
-   Vein relationship: crosses_or_ignores_veins

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location:
    leaf_blade_with_possible_stem_pod_and_petiole_involvement

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   scattered_necrotic_leaf_lesions
-   lesions_on_multiple_above_ground_plant_parts
-   lesion_coalescence
-   progressive_leaf_drying
-   pod_or_stem_lesions_in_affected_plants

## Hallmark Features

-   circular_to_oval_necrotic_lesions
-   tan_brown_centers
-   dark_brown_to_reddish_brown_margins
-   discrete_scattered_distribution
-   possible_multiple_above_ground_plant_part_involvement

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   contrasting_yellow_green_mosaic_without_necrotic_lesions
-   predominantly_gray_angular_leaf_spots
-   white_powdery_surface_growth
-   raised_orange_brown_pustules
-   continuous_uniform_leaf_yellowing

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Green Gram Yellow Mosaic Disease
-   Green Gram Cercospora Leaf Spot
-   bacterial_leaf_spot
-   other fungal leaf spots

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare lesion geometry and center-to-margin colors before
    interpreting the disease pattern.
-   Distinguish tan-brown necrotic lesions from yellow-green mosaic
    discoloration.
-   Compare lesion shape and margin definition with Cercospora-type leaf
    spots.
-   Check whether symptoms extend beyond the leaf blade to stems,
    petioles, or pods.
-   Treat uncertainty in plant-part involvement or lesion boundaries as
    uncertainty, not as a fact.
