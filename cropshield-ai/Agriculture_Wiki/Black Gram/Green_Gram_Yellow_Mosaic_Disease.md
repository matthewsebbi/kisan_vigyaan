# Green Gram Yellow Mosaic Disease

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   diffuse_discoloration
-   mosaic_pattern

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `irregular_mosaic_patches`
-   Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
-   Width profile: `variable`
-   Ends: `irregular`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small yellow patches may enlarge and merge into extensive chlorotic
    areas; multiple leaves can be affected

-   Center / primary color: bright_yellow, yellow, yellow_green
-   Margin: green_to_yellow_green

### Spatial Location

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   yellow_and_green_mosaic_foliage
-   multiple_affected_leaves
-   reduced_green_leaf_area
-   stunting_in_severe_cases

## Hallmark Features

-   contrasting_yellow_and_green_mosaic_pattern
-   irregular_chlorotic_patches
-   progressive_mosaic_development
-   multiple_leaf_involvement
-   possible_whole_plant_stunting_in_severe_infection

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   discrete_brown_necrotic_leaf_spots
-   dark_brown_lesions_with_distinct_margins
-   white_powdery_surface_growth
-   raised_orange_brown_pustules
-   elongated_linear_necrotic_blight

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Green Gram Cercospora Leaf Spot
-   Green Gram Anthracnose
-   Nutrient deficiency
-   Other mosaic or viral diseases

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare the overall mosaic pattern before interpreting individual
    yellow areas as lesions.
-   Distinguish diffuse yellow-green mosaic discoloration from discrete
    necrotic leaf spots.
-   Compare center-to-margin color relationships rather than color words
    in isolation.
-   Distinguish continuous or mosaic discoloration from raised pustules
    or surface fungal growth.
-   Treat uncertainty in mosaic pattern or symptom distribution as
    uncertainty, not as a fact.
