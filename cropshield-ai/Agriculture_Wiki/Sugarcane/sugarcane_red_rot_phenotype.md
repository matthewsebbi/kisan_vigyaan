# Sugarcane Red Rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- stalk_internal
- leaf_midrib
- leaf_blade
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- internal_tissue_discoloration
- midrib_lesion
- foliar_wilt_and_drying

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `elongated_streak_on_midrib_transverse_white_spots_internally`
- Elongation: `high_along_stalk_and_midrib`
- Width profile: `variable`
- Ends: `tapered`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_internally_discrete_to_coalescing_on_midrib`

### Size

- Midrib lesions: 2 mm to several centimeters in length
- Internal stalk rot: can span multiple internodes longitudinally

### Color

- Center / primary color: bright_red_to_dark_red_with_transverse_white_patches
- Margin: dark_red_to_brown
- Surrounding tissue: pale_yellow_to_straw_colored
- Color transition: `distinct`
- Current/overall colors: red, dark_red, reddish_brown, white, yellow

### Texture

- Current texture: soft_and_juicy_early, dry_pithy_and_hollow_late, brittle
- Early appearance: `water_soaked_reddening`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `longitudinal`
- Distribution: `internal_nodal_and_internodal_spread`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `midrib_parallel`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `internal_stalk_tissues_and_leaf_midrib`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- top_leaf_yellowing_and_wilting
- drying_of_crown_leaves
- stalk_shrinkage_and_shriveling
- alcoholic_or_acidic_fermentation_odor_on_splitting
- total_crop_lodging_and_death

## Hallmark Features

- internal_red_stalk_tissue_interspersed_with_transverse_white_spots_or_bands
- sour_alcoholic_fermentation_odor_when_stalk_is_split
- blood_red_lesions_with_dark_margins_on_leaf_midribs
- sudden_drying_and_wilting_of_the_third_or_fourth_leaf_from_crown

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- whip_like_black_sorus
- excessive_bushy_grassy_tillering
- ladder_like_internal_cavities_without_reddening
- distorted_twisted_top_leaves_pokkah_boeng_wrinkling

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Sugarcane Wilt (*Fusarium sacchari*)
- Pokkah Boeng (*Fusarium* species)
- Pine-apple Disease (*Ceratocystis paradoxa*)

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Red rot uniquely exhibits transverse white spots or bands across the red discolored internal stalk tissue.
- Split stalks emit a distinct alcoholic or sour fermentation odor in Red Rot.
- Red Rot features distinct red lesions on leaf midribs, whereas Wilt mainly causes internal pith discoloration without midrib lesions.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 25°C - 32°C (Optimal for fungal proliferation)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (>80%)

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Heavy rainfall, waterlogging, high soil moisture, and flooding favor disease development and spore dissemination.

### Soil

- Poorly drained, waterlogged soils or heavy clay soils with poor aeration.

## Crop Stage

- Mid-crop stage to maturity (Grand growth phase to ripening stage)
