# Sugarcane Pokkah Boeng

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- top_leaves
- spindle_leaf
- stalk_top_growing_point

### Symptom Class

- chlorosis
- leaf_distortion_and_wrinkling
- top_rot

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_wrinkled_distortion_and_ladder_like_lesions`
- Elongation: `variable`
- Width profile: `irregular`
- Ends: `irregular`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_confluent`

### Size

- Distortions cover entire leaf base; necrotic cut-marks range 1 cm to several centimeters

### Color

- Center / primary color: pale_yellow_chlorosis_turning_reddish_brown_necrotic
- Margin: dark_red_to_brown
- Surrounding tissue: green
- Color transition: `gradual_to_distinct`
- Current/overall colors: chlorotic_yellow, red, reddish_brown, dark_brown

### Texture

- Current texture: malformed, crumpled, leathery, wet_soft_rot_in_advanced_stage
- Early appearance: `chlorotic_whitish_yellow_base_of_spindle_leaf`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `transverse_knife_cut_marks_and_longitudinal_wrinkling`
- Distribution: `apical_crown_leaves`
- Coalescence: `moderate`
- Vein relationship: `crosses_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `base_of_young_spindle_leaves_and_crown`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- chlorotic_whitish_yellowing_at_base_of_top_leaves
- crumpled_twisted_and_wrinkled_spindle_leaves
- ladder_like_transverse_cuts_knife_cut_symptom_on_stalk
- shortening_and_rotting_of_top_internodes_top_rot
- death_of_growing_point_and_side_shoot_sprouting

## Hallmark Features

- pronounced_wrinkling_crumpling_and_twisting_of_young_top_leaves
- knife_cut_transverse_incisions_on_stalk_internodes
- transition_from_chlorosis_at_leaf_base_to_top_rot_and_apical_dieback

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- black_sooty_whip_structures
- transverse_white_spots_inside_red_split_stalk
- albino_grassy_tiller_bush
- punctate_vascular_dots_confined_only_to_nodes

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Mechanical/Herbicide injury (e.g., glyphosate drift causing twisted tops)
- Sugarcane Red Rot
- Top Borer damage

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Pokkah Boeng presents characteristic "knife-cut" transverse slits on the stem alongside crumpled, malformed crown leaves.
- Unlike herbicide damage, Pokkah Boeng progresses to fungal top rot with dark red necrotic patches and shoot decay.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 22°C - 30°C

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (>80%) combined with overcast rainy weather.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Sudden onset of monsoon rains following warm dry periods triggers rapid spore germination and infection.

### Soil

- Soils with high nitrogen levels promoting rapid, succulent plant growth.

## Crop Stage

- Grand growth stage (early fast-growing summer/monsoon phase)
