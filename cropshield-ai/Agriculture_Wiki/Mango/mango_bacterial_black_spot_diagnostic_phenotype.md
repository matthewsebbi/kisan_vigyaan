# Mango Bacterial Black Spot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- leaf_petiole
- young_twigs
- fruit_exocarp
- pedicel

### Symptom Class

- angular_leaf_spot
- raised_corky_lesion
- fruit_canker
- twig_canker

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `angular_vein_bound_spots_to_star_shaped_cracked_cankers`
- Elongation: `moderate_along_fruit_cracks_and_leaf_veins`
- Width profile: `raised_prominent`
- Ends: `abrupt`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_spots_coalescing_into_deep_cracks`

### Size

- Leaf spots: 1-4 mm; Fruit lesions: 2-15 mm, cracking open up to several millimeters deep.

### Color

- Center / primary color: black, purplish_black
- Margin: dark_brown, blackish
- Surrounding tissue: yellow_chlorotic_halo
- Color transition: `abrupt`
- Current/overall colors: black, dark_brown, yellow, pale_white_bacterial_exudate

### Texture

- Current texture: raised, corky, rough, cracked, gummy, exuding_sticky_gummy_substance
- Early appearance: `small_water_soaked_angular_clear_spots_on_underside_of_leaves`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `constrained_by_leaf_veins_star_burst_on_fruit`
- Distribution: `denser_along_leaf_midribs_and_windward_fruit_surfaces`
- Coalescence: `frequently_coalescing_into_large_corky_cratered_cankers`
- Vein relationship: `strictly_constrained_by_veinlets_on_leaves`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `mature_leaves_twigs_and_developing_fruits`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- angular_black_leaf_spots_with_yellow_halo
- longitudinal_cankers_on_petioles_and_twigs
- star_shaped_deeply_cracked_fruit_cankers
- gummy_bacterial_exudate_on_fruit_and_stems
- severe_defoliation_and_premature_fruit_drop

## Hallmark Features

- angular_water_soaked_leaf_spots_strictly_delimited_by_leaf_veinlets
- raised_black_corky_fruit_lesions_splitting_open_in_star_burst_patterns
- sticky_gummy_bacterial_exudate_bleeding_from_fruit_and_stem_cankers
- longitudinal_splitting_and_cankers_on_twigs_and_petioles
- yellow_chlorotic_halo_surrounding_angular_black_leaf_lesions

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- smooth_sunken_fruit_lesions_with_pink_salmon_spore_masses
- white_powdery_coating_on_leaves_or_inflorescences
- hyperbranched_bunchy_rosette_panicles
- leaf_shot_holes_without_corky_vein_bound_borders

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Mango Anthracnose (*Colletotrichum gloeosporioides*)
- Mango Scab (*Elsinoë mangiferae*)
- Wind scuffing / Mechanical injury

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Lesion relief and cracking: Bacterial Black Spot lesions are distinctly raised, corky, and crack open in a star-shaped pattern with gummy exudate; Anthracnose lesions are smooth, flat, or sunken without gumming.
- Leaf spot boundary: Bacterial Black Spot leaf lesions are strictly angular and vein-limited; Anthracnose leaf lesions are often circular to irregular and drop out (shot-hole).
- Exudate presence: Bacterial Black Spot produces sticky exudate droplets on lesions under wet/humid conditions, containing bacterial cells.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 25°C to 32°C (Favors bacterial multiplication and spread)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (> 85% RH)

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Rainstorms, wind-driven rain, and high winds cause micro-wounds and splash bacteria across canopy

### Soil

- Non-specific; windbreak presence reduces storm-driven physical damage and disease incidence

## Crop Stage

- Active flush stage, twig elongation, and fruit development up to harvest.
