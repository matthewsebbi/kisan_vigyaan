# Mango Powdery Mildew

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- inflorescence_panicle
- leaf_blade
- young_fruit
- floral_axis

### Symptom Class

- superficial_powdery_growth
- chlorotic_mottling
- flower_abortion
- fruit_russeting

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `patchy_circular_to_irregular_powdery_mats`
- Elongation: `low_radial_expansion`
- Width profile: `broad_coating`
- Ends: `diffuse`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_patches_coalescing_into_continuous_mats`

### Size

- Patches 2 mm to full coverage of whole panicles, leaves, and young fruitlets

### Color

- Center / primary color: white, off_white, light_grey
- Margin: translucent_white
- Surrounding tissue: chlorotic_yellow, purplish_brown_necrotic
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: white, grey, ash_grey, brown, dark_brown

### Texture

- Current texture: powdery, dusty, velvety, dry, necrotic_and_cracked_in_late_stages
- Early appearance: `white_dusty_pinpoint_patches_on_inflorescence_and_underside_of_leaves`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `non_directional_radial_expansion`
- Distribution: `widespread_over_inflorescences_and_young_foliage`
- Coalescence: `extensive_covering_entire_panicles`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable_superficial`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `young_inflorescences_blossom_buds_and_underside_of_tender_leaves`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- white_powdery_coating_on_panicles
- massive_flower_and_fruitlet_drop
- greyish_brown_necrotic_panicle_skeletons
- corky_russeted_cracked_young_fruits
- leaf_curling_and_distortion

## Hallmark Features

- white_to_ash_grey_powdery_superficial_mycelial_growth
- total_blossom_infection_leading_to_failure_of_fruit_set
- premature_drop_of_infected_young_peaberry_stage_fruits
- corky_scarring_and_cracking_on_surviving_growing_fruit
- distortion_and_under_leaf_whitening_of_young_flush

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- sunken_black_lesions_with_salmon_pink_spore_droplets
- star_shaped_cracked_black_lesions_with_bacterial_exudate
- vegetative_or_floral_hypertrophic_bunchy_malformation
- internal_vascular_reddish_brown_streaking

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Mango Anthracnose (*Colletotrichum gloeosporioides*)
- Mango Malformation Disease (*Fusarium mangiferae*)
- Sooty Mold (*Capnodium* spp.)

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Fungal growth appearance: Powdery Mildew produces a distinct white, easily wiped off powdery coating; Sooty mold is dark black and velvety; Anthracnose lacks white powdery coating.
- Effect on flowers: Powdery Mildew causes dry necrosis and drop of flowers leaving bare main axes; Malformation causes thickened, overcrowded green floral structures.
- Environmental requirement: Powdery mildew thrives in dry, cool-to-warm nights without requiring heavy rain, unlike Anthracnose which requires free water.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 20°C to 25°C (Favors conidial germination and rapid disease development)

### Humidity

- High atmospheric humidity (65% to 80% RH) aids conidial germination without free water

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Dry weather with cool nights and heavy dew favors infection; heavy rainfall actually washes off spores and reduces disease spread

### Soil

- Non-specific; dense canopies with poor airflow exacerbate microclimate humidity

## Crop Stage

- Flowering and early fruit-set stages (peaberry stage) are most critical.
