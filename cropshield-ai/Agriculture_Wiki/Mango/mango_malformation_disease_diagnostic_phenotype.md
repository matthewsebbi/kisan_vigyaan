# Mango Malformation Disease

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- vegetative_shoot_apices
- inflorescence_panicle
- axillary_buds
- young_twigs

### Symptom Class

- floral_malformation
- vegetative_malformation
- shoot_hypertrophy
- witchs_broom

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `compact_bunchy_hyperplastic_mass`
- Elongation: `extremely_shortened_internodes`
- Width profile: `thickened_swollen_axis`
- Ends: `not_applicable`
- Discrete or continuous: `localized_clusters_or_whole_panicle_transformation`

### Size

- Malformed panicles are 2 to 5 times denser and more compact than normal open panicles; vegetative shoots form tight bunches 5-15 cm wide.

### Color

- Center / primary color: green, pale_green, yellowish_green
- Margin: green
- Surrounding tissue: green
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: light_green, yellow_green, dull_brown_when_necrotic

### Texture

- Current texture: fleshy, thickened, brittle, overcrowded, rigid
- Early appearance: `swollen_axillary_buds_producing_shortened_compact_shoots`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `clustered_at_branch_apices`
- Distribution: `randomly_distributed_in_canopy_branches`
- Coalescence: `dense_massing_of_floral_and_vegetative_structures`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `terminal_and_axillary_buds_of_flowering_branches`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- compact_bunchy_leaf_rosettes ("witch's broom")
- hypertrophied_thickened_branched_panicles
- sterile_flowers_with_increased_number_of_male_flowers
- failure_to_set_fruit_on_affected_panicles
- persistence_of_dried_mummified_malformed_panicles

## Hallmark Features

- hyperbranching_and_thickened_floral_axes_forming_compact_heads
- proliferation_of_enlarged_male_flowers_with_greatly_reduced_hermaphrodite_flowers
- extreme_internode_shortening_in_vegetative_shoots_forming_bunchy_top_rosettes
- total_lack_of_fruit_setting_on_malformed_panicles
- dark_brown_drying_and_long_term_persistence_of_dead_panicles_on_trees

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- superficial_white_powdery_fungal_coating_on_normal_shaped_panicles
- tear_stain_black_lesions_on_fruit
- star_shaped_corky_cracked_leaf_or_fruit_spots
- rapid_wilting_and_vascular_browning_of_main_trunk

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Mango Bud Mite damage (*Aceria mangiferae*)
- Zinc deficiency (causes little leaf, but lacks massive floral hypertrophy)
- Growth regulator / hormone imbalance

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Morphological structure: Malformation causes true structural hypertrophy with thickened, fleshy, overcrowded panicle branches; mite damage alone causes bud scales to turn dark/warty without massive floral panicle enlargement.
- Absence of fungal coatings: Does not have white powder (Powdery Mildew) or sunken black spots (Anthracnose).
- Fruit yield: Malformed panicles are completely sterile and set no fruit.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 15°C to 25°C during bud initiation favors fungal colonization and hormonal disruption

### Humidity

- Moderate to high humidity favors spread via vector (mango bud mite)

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Not strictly moisture-dependent; transmitted systemically and vector-assisted by mites

### Soil

- Non-specific; affects trees regardless of soil type

## Crop Stage

- Vegetative bud break (vegetative form) and floral bud initiation/anthesis (floral form).
