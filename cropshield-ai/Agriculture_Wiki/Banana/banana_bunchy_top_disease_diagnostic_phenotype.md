# Banana Bunchy Top Disease

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- leaf_petiole
- crown_rosette
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- viral_stunting
- leaf_rosetting
- chlorotic_streak

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `dark_green_dot_dash_line_streak`
- Elongation: `high_along_leaf_veins`
- Width profile: `narrow_linear`
- Ends: `abrupt`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_dots_forming_intermittent_lines`

### Size

- Dark green streaks ("Morse code" pattern) 1-15 mm long along secondary leaf veins; severely reduced overall leaf size and internode length

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_green
- Margin: chlorotic_yellow
- Surrounding tissue: green
- Color transition: `abrupt`
- Current/overall colors: dark_green, yellow, pale_green, necrotic_brown_margins

### Texture

- Current texture: stiff, brittle, erect, leathery, crumpled
- Early appearance: `dark_green_dots_or_short_streaks_on_midrib_and_petiole`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `parallel_to_secondary_veins_and_down_petiole`
- Distribution: `concentrated_on_leaf_blade_base_and_petiole`
- Coalescence: `clustered_at_plant_apices`
- Vein relationship: `strictly_follows_secondary_leaf_venation`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `youngest_emerging_leaves_at_the_crown`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- severe_plant_stunting
- crowded_rosette_of_leaves_at_pseudostem_apex ("bunchy top")
- narrow_erect_short_leaves_with_wavy_yellow_margins
- dark_green_dot_dash_streaks_along_veins ("Morse code" pattern)
- failure_of_infected_plants_to_produce_bunches

## Hallmark Features

- morse_code_dark_green_dot_and_dash_streaks_on_leaf_veins_and_petiole
- bunchy_rosette_appearance_of_crowded_erect_leaves_at_crown
- stiff_brittle_leathery_leaves_with_wavy_chlorotic_margins
- extreme_internode_shortening_and_overall_stunting
- complete_loss_of_fruit_production_if_infected_early

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- internal_vascular_red_or_brown_discoloration
- bacterial_ooze_from_pseudostem
- large_elliptic_necrotic_lesions_with_ash_grey_centers
- skirt_like_leaf_collapse_around_pseudostem_base

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Banana Mosaic Virus / Cucumber Mosaic Virus (CMV)
- Zinc or Nitrogen deficiency
- Growth regulator / Herbicide damage

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- "Morse Code" Streaking: Look for distinct dark green dots and dashes along secondary veins and down the petiole ("J hook" pattern near midrib), unique to BBTV.
- Rosette Architecture: BBTV causes severe, tight rosetting ("bunchy apex") with stiff, erect leaves; mosaic virus causes chlorotic mottling without dense bunching.
- Petiole/Vascular Integrity: BBTV has clean vascular structures without rot or discoloration inside the pseudostem.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 25°C to 30°C (Favors banana aphid *Pentalonia nigronervosa* vector reproduction and activity)

### Humidity

- Warm, moist conditions encourage vector populations and rapid viral expression

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Intermittent rainfall promotes lush vegetative flushes that attract aphid vectors

### Soil

- Non-specific; viral systemics operate independently of soil type, though rich soils accelerate growth symptom visibility

## Crop Stage

- All crop stages; young plants infected early are completely stunted and fail to produce fruit, while plants infected later yield distorted, non-commercial bunches.
