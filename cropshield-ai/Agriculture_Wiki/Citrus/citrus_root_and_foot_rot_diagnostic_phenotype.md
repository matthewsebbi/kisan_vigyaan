# Citrus Root / Foot Rot (*Phytophthora nicotianae* / *Phytophthora citrophthora*)

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- feeder_roots
- tap_root
- crown_collar_region

### Symptom Class

- root_decay_and_bark_sloughing

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `diffuse_root_lesions_and_girdling_crown_canker`
- Elongation: `variable`
- Width profile: `encircling`
- Ends: `indistinct`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_decay`

### Size

- Spans entire feeder root systems or girdles root crown collar.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown_to_black_decayed_tissue
- Margin: water_soaked_brown
- Surrounding tissue: sloughed_off_white_vascular_core
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: black_root_rot, brown, yellow_foliage

### Texture

- Current texture: soft, watery, sloughing_root_cortex, stringy_bare_stele
- Early appearance: `water_soaked_feeder_roots`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `diffuse_root_system`
- Distribution: `underground_spreading_upward`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `feeder_roots_tap_root_and_collar`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- sloughing_of_root_cortex_leaving_bare_vascular_thread
- destruction_of_feeder_roots
- crown_foot_rot_canker_near_soil_line
- foliar_yellowing_along_veins
- leaf_drop_and_canopy_thinning

## Hallmark Features

- root_cortex_sloughs_easily_leaving_bare_fibrous_stele
- water_soaked_foul_decay_of_feeder_roots
- girdling_foot_rot_lesions_at_soil_line
- yellowing_of_foliar_veins_due_to_root_starvation

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present:

- raised_corky_canker_spots_on_leaves
- wood_stem_pitting
- asymmetrical_blotchy_leaf_mottle
- powdery_white_spore_mats

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Nematode Damage (*Tylenchulus semipenetrans*)
- Citrus Gummosis
- Waterlogging / Oxygen Starvation

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Nematode damage causes soil particles to cling tightly to dirty roots ("dirty root" appearance) without soft cortical sloughing.
- Waterlogging rots roots uniformly due to lack of oxygen without specific brown lesion progression on crown bark.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- High soil temperatures (28°C–32°C) favor *P. nicotianae* root rot.

### Humidity

- Soil saturation (> 90% water-filled pore space) stimulates sporangia release.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Poor drainage, over-irrigation, or standing water around root crown drives disease.

### Soil

- Fine-textured clay soils with poor internal drainage are most conducive.

## Crop Stage

- Nursery seedlings to mature orchards under wet soil conditions.
