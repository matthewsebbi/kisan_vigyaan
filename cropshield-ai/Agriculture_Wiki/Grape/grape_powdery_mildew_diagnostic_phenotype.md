# Grape Powdery Mildew

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- green_shoots
- flower_clusters
- rachis
- berries

### Symptom Class

- powdery_surface_coating
- foliar_chlorosis_and_distortion
- berry_splitting_and_russeting
- web_like_shooting_canes_necrosis

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `circular_patches_expanding_into_diffuse_epiphytic_mats`
- Elongation: `low_isodiametric_expansion`
- Width profile: `continuous_over_surface`
- Ends: `diffuse_and_feathery`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_spots_coalescing_into_continuous_powder`

### Size

- Patches start as 2–10 mm circular powdery spots and coalesce to cover entire leaves, canes, or berry clusters.

### Color

- Center / primary color: ash_grey_to_powdery_white
- Margin: diffuse_chlorotic_yellow_ halo
- Surrounding tissue: dark_green_to_stunted_reddish_green
- Color transition: `gradual_and_diffuse`
- Current/overall colors: white, grey, web_like_dark_brown_shoot_scarring, reddish_black

### Texture

- Current texture: dusty, powdery, fine_webby_mycelium, dry, brittle
- Early appearance: `dull_whitish_grey_dusty_patch_on_upper_or_lower_leaf`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `radial_expansion_from_infection_point`
- Distribution: `widespread_across_all_green_tissues`
- Coalescence: `extensive_covering_entire_canopy_and_fruit`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable_grows_epiphytically_over_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `adaxial_and_abaxial_leaf_surfaces_stems_and_fruit_epidermis`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- dusty_ash_grey_coating_on_leaves_and_fruit
- strong_musty_moldy_odor_in_infected_canopy
- berry_skin_splitting_exposing_seeds
- dark_reddish_brown_web_like_scarring_on_dormant_canes
- stunted_leaf_growth_and_curling

## Hallmark Features

- dusty_ash_white_to_grey_powdery_growth_on_both_leaf_surfaces_and_fruit
- distinct_musty_fungal_odor_in_severely_infected_vineyard_rows
- berry_growth_stunting_leading_to_skin_cracking_and_seed_exposure
- reddish_brown_branching_scar_lines_on_canes_after_infection_clears

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present:

- sporulation_strictly_confined_to_the_lower_leaf_surface_with_upper_oil_spots
- deep_sunken_crater_like_cankers_with_raised_red_margins
- clear_amber_resin_bleeding_from_trunk_wounds
- slimy_pinkish_spore_masses_oozing_from_sunken_fruit_rottens

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Grape Downy Mildew (*Plasmopara viticola*)
- Dust / Spray residue accumulation
- Botrytis bunch rot (early stage)

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Powdery mildew colonizes **both upper and lower** leaf surfaces and rubs off as a fine dry powder, whereas Downy Mildew sporulation is strictly on the **lower** surface under oily upper spots.
- Powdery mildew prefers shaded, humid microclimates **without requiring free rain water**, unlike Downy Mildew which depends on free water for spore germination.
- Spray residues wipe off without leaving web-like dark brown scarring underneath, which is characteristic of powdery mildew mycelial attachments.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 15°C – 32°C (Optimal 20°C–27°C; growth ceases above 35°C).

### Humidity

- Medium to high relative humidity (40%–100%) favors conidial germination.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- **Does NOT require free water** to infect. Rain actually washes conidia off leaves and reduces spore germination. High shade and stagnant air promote disease.

### Soil

- Dense canopy growth driven by excess soil nitrogen creates ideal shaded microclimates for infection.

## Crop Stage

- All green stages: shoot emergence, pre-bloom, flowering, fruit set, and pre-veraison.
