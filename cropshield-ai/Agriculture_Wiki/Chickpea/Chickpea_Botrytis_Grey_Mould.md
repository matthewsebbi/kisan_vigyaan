# Chickpea Botrytis Grey Mould

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   petiole
-   stem
-   flower
-   pod
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   water_soaked_lesions
-   necrotic_lesions
-   superficial_fungal_growth
-   grey_mould

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `irregular_water_soaked_to_necrotic_lesions`
-   Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
-   Width profile: `variable`
-   Ends: `irregular`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small water-soaked lesions enlarge rapidly and may merge into
    extensive necrotic areas covered by gray mould growth

### Color

-   Center / primary color: water_soaked, pale_brown, brown, gray_brown
-   Margin: brown, dark_brown
-   Surrounding tissue: green
-   Color transition: `gradual_to_sharp`
-   Current/overall colors: water_soaked, pale_brown, brown, gray_brown,
    dark_brown, gray

### Texture

-   Current texture: water_soaked, soft, moist, necrotic,
    gray_fuzzy_mould, brittle_in_severe_stage
-   Early appearance: `small_water_soaked_spots`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: `irregular`
-   Distribution: `irregular_scattered_to_coalescing`
-   Coalescence: `extensive`
-   Vein relationship: `crosses_or_ignores_veins`

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location:
    `leaf_blade_with_possible_flower_stem_and_pod_involvement`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   water_soaked_lesions
-   gray_fuzzy_mould_growth
-   rapid_lesion_coalescence
-   flower_and_pod_infection
-   stem_and_leaf_necrosis
-   extensive_blight
-   plant_decline_in_severe_cases

## Hallmark Features

-   water_soaked_to_brown_necrotic_lesions
-   gray_fuzzy_mould_growth
-   rapid_coalescence_of_lesions
-   infection_of_flowers_stems_and_pods
-   extensive_soft_necrotic_blight

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   dry_circular_lesions_with_distinct_dark_margins_without_gray_mould
-   dry_shriveled_root_decay
-   primary_vascular_browning
-   bright_yellow_green_mosaic_pattern
-   white_powdery_surface_growth

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Chickpea Ascochyta Blight
-   Chickpea Anthracnose
-   Chickpea Dry Root Rot
-   Chickpea Fusarium Wilt

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare soft water-soaked lesions and gray fuzzy mould with dry
    discrete leaf spots.
-   Look for characteristic gray superficial mould growth on affected
    tissues.
-   Check for flower, stem, and pod involvement when distinguishing from
    primarily foliar diseases.
-   Compare tissue consistency, especially moist and soft versus dry and
    papery.
-   Treat uncertainty in mould growth or tissue moisture as uncertainty,
    not as a fact.
