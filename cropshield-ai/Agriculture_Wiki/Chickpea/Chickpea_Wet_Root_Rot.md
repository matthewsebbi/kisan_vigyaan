# Chickpea Wet Root Rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   root
-   collar
-   lower_stem
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   water_soaked_root_rot
-   necrotic_lesions
-   plant_wilting

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `irregular_water_soaked_root_and_collar_lesions`
-   Elongation: `moderate_along_root_and_lower_stem`
-   Width profile: `variable`
-   Ends: `irregular`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small water-soaked areas on roots or collar may enlarge and merge,
    affecting progressively larger portions of the root and lower stem
    tissues

### Color

-   Center / primary color: water_soaked, pale_brown, brown, dark_brown
-   Margin: brown, dark_brown
-   Surrounding tissue: pale_green_to_healthy_tissue
-   Color transition: `gradual_to_sharp`
-   Current/overall colors: water_soaked, pale_brown, brown, dark_brown,
    yellow_green

### Texture

-   Current texture: water_soaked, soft, moist, mushy, necrotic
-   Early appearance: `water_soaked_root_or_collar_tissue`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: `irregular`
-   Distribution: `root_and_collar_rot`
-   Coalescence: `moderate_to_extensive`
-   Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location: `roots_and_collar_region`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   rapid_wilting
-   leaf_yellowing
-   drooping_foliage
-   collar_tissue_decay
-   root_decay
-   reduced_plant_vigor
-   plant_death_in_severe_cases

## Hallmark Features

-   water_soaked_root_or_collar_tissue
-   soft_to_mushy_decay
-   brown_to_dark_brown_rot
-   rapid_wilting
-   progressive_root_and_collar_tissue_breakdown

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   dry_brittle_shriveled_root_tissue
-   primary_vascular_browning_without_external_root_decay
-   white_powdery_leaf_growth
-   discrete_circular_leaf_spots
-   bright_yellow_green_mosaic_pattern

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Chickpea Fusarium Wilt
-   Chickpea Dry Root Rot
-   Collar Rot
-   Other Chickpea Root Rots

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare soft water-soaked decay with dry brittle root rot.
-   Inspect the collar and root tissue directly before interpreting
    above-ground wilting.
-   Distinguish external tissue breakdown from internal vascular
    discoloration.
-   Compare tissue consistency, especially moist and mushy versus dry
    and brittle.
-   Treat uncertainty in root moisture, softness, or vascular
    discoloration as uncertainty, not as a fact.
