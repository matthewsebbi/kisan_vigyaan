# Chickpea Ascochyta Blight

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   petiole
-   stem
-   pod
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   discrete_lesions
-   necrotic_spots
-   blight

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `circular_to_oval_lesions`
-   Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
-   Width profile: `wider_at_center`
-   Ends: `rounded_to_irregular`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small circular to oval lesions that enlarge and may merge, producing
    larger necrotic areas on leaves, stems, petioles, or pods

### Color

-   Center / primary color: tan, pale_brown, gray_brown, dark_brown
-   Margin: dark_brown, reddish_brown
-   Surrounding tissue: green
-   Color transition: `sharp_two_tone`
-   Current/overall colors: tan, pale_brown, gray_brown, dark_brown,
    reddish_brown

### Texture

-   Current texture: dry_papery, slightly_rough, necrotic,
    brittle_in_severe_stage
-   Early appearance: `small_pale_to_water_soaked_spots`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: `irregular`
-   Distribution: `discrete_scattered_to_coalescing`
-   Coalescence: `moderate_to_extensive`
-   Vein relationship: `crosses_or_ignores_veins`

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location: `leaf_blade_with_possible_petioles_stems_and_pods`

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Chickpea Botrytis Grey Mould
-   Chickpea Anthracnose
-   Chickpea Dry Root Rot
-   Chickpea Fusarium Wilt

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare discrete circular or oval lesions with diffuse wilting or
    root-rot symptoms.
-   Compare lesion center and margin color relationships rather than
    color words alone.
-   Check whether lesions occur across leaves, stems, petioles, and
    pods.
-   Distinguish dry necrotic lesions from gray fuzzy mould growth.
-   Treat uncertainty in lesion identification or plant-part involvement
    as uncertainty, not as a fact.
