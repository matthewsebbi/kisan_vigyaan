# Groundnut Collar rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- collar
- crown
- lower_stem
- roots
- foliage

### Symptom Class

- collar_rot
- basal_tissue_decay
- wilt_and_dieback

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_collar_lesion`
- Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
- Width profile: `localized_around_collar`
- Ends: `irregular`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

- lesions are concentrated around the collar and may enlarge to encircle or extensively damage basal tissue
- above-ground symptoms can become extensive as basal tissues are compromised

### Color

- Center / primary color: `brown_to_dark_brown`
- Margin: `brown`
- Surrounding tissue: green initially, later yellowing and drying
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: tan, brown, dark_brown, yellow, dry_brown

### Texture

- Current texture: rotted, softened_or_weakened, dry_in_advanced_stage
- Early appearance: `basal_discoloration_or_small_collar_lesion`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `around_collar`
- Distribution: `localized_basal_rot`
- Coalescence: `can_progress_to_extensive_collar_decay`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `collar_at_or_near_soil_line`
- Secondary locations: lower_stem, crown, roots, lower foliage

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- sudden_or_progressive_wilting
- yellowing_of_foliage
- drooping_of_leaflets
- branch_dieback
- basal_tissue_decay
- premature_drying
- plant_death_in_severe_cases

## Hallmark Features

- primary_rot_centered_on_the_collar_or_crown_region
- brown_to_dark_brown_basal_tissue_discoloration
- wilting_above_the_affected_collar
- progressive_collar_decay_can_interrupt_water_and_nutrient_supply
- severe_infection_can_lead_to_whole_plant_death

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- primary_leaf_spots_without_collar_or_basal_tissue_involvement
- generalized_wilt_without_visible_or_examinable_collar_involvement
- primary_stem_lesion_far_above_the_collar
- rust_pustules_as_the_dominant_symptom
- root_rot_without_collar_or_crown_symptoms

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Groundnut Wilt
- Groundnut Stem rot
- Groundnut Root rot
- Groundnut Early leaf spot
- Groundnut Late leaf spot

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Identify the affected plant part before interpreting color.
- A lesion centered at the collar or crown is more informative than generalized wilting alone.
- Distinguish collar decay from a lesion that begins higher on the stem.
- Compare the external basal lesion with any internal vascular discoloration before classifying the disease as a vascular wilt.
- Examine the soil-line region carefully because collar symptoms may be obscured by soil or plant debris.
- Treat absence of a visible collar lesion cautiously when the basal tissue has not been examined directly.
