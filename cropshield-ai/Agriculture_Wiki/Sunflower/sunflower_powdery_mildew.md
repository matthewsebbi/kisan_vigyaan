# Sunflower Powdery Mildew

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- stem
- bract

### Symptom Class

- powdery_surface_growth
- chlorosis
- premature_senescence

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_to_circular_patches`
- Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
- Width profile: `variable`
- Ends: `not_applicable`
- Discrete or continuous: `coalescing_patches`

### Size

- Starts as small discrete spots (2-5 mm) expanding into continuous powdery white coatings covering entire leaf blades

### Color

- Center / primary color: white_to_ash_gray (talcum_powder_like)
- Margin: ill_defined
- Surrounding tissue: pale_green_to_yellow (chlorotic)
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: white, gray, yellow, brown_necrotic_in_late_stage

### Texture

- Current texture: powdery, dusty, dry, brittle_in_severe_stage
- Early appearance: `subtle_white_powdery_tufts`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `random`
- Distribution: `scattered_patches_coalescing_to_full_coverage`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `independent_of_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `upper_leaf_surface_first_spreading_to_lower_surface_and_stems`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- white_powdery_appearance_on_foliage
- upper_canopy_chlorosis
- premature_leaf_defoliation
- reduced_seed_filling

## Hallmark Features

- talcum_powder_like_white_mycelial_growth
- upper_leaf_surface_predominance
- coalescing_white_patches
- dark_cleistothecia_fruiting_bodies_in_late_season

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- downy_tufts_confined_to_lower_leaf_surface (indicates Downy Mildew)
- rust_colored_pustules (indicates Rust)
- water_soaked_soft_rot (indicates Sclerotinia)

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Sunflower Downy Mildew
- Sunflower Rust
- Chemical residue / dust accumulation

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Powdery mildew appears primarily on the upper leaf surface as a dry dusty coating; downy mildew sporulation occurs on the abaxial (lower) leaf surface with upper surface chlorosis matching veins.
- Powdery growth rubs off easily leaving chlorotic tissue underneath.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Warm temperatures (20°C - 28°C)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity favors conidial germination, though free water on leaves inhibits spore germination

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Dry canopy favored; rainfall can wash off spores

### Soil

- Excess nitrogen fertilizer increases canopy density and susceptibility

## Crop Stage

- Flowering to seed maturity (R5 to R9 stages)
