# Sunflower Root Rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- root_system
- basal_stem_crown
- taproot
- lateral_roots

### Symptom Class

- root_decay_and_necrosis
- basal_crown_rot
- vascular_wilt
- stunting_and_damping_off

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_decaying_zones`
- Elongation: `high_along_root_axis`
- Width profile: `variable_expanding`
- Ends: `diffuse`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_root_decay`

### Size

- Can range from small, discrete root tip lesions (1–5 mm) to total taproot decay extending 10–30 cm along the root system and up into the basal crown.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown, reddish_brown, charcoal_black
- Margin: dark_brown_to_black
- Surrounding tissue: pale_yellow_to_chlorotic
- Color transition: `gradual_to_diffuse`
- Current/overall colors: dark_brown, black, ash_gray, bleached_white_near_crown

### Texture

- Current texture: soft, water_soaked, sloughing_root_cortex, dry_spongy_or_shredded_in_charcoal_rot
- Early appearance: `soft_water_soaked_brown_root_tips`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `longitudinal_along_roots`
- Distribution: `generalized_root_system_decay`
- Coalescence: `extensive_covering_entire_root_mass`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `below_ground_root_system_and_sub_surface_crown`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- sudden_wilting_and_epinasty
- seedling_damping_off
- lower_leaf_yellowing_and_senescence
- easy_uprooting_due_to_root_loss
- patch_distribution_in_low_or_waterlogged_field_areas

## Hallmark Features

- primary_decay_and_discoloration_restricted_to_roots_and_taproot
- cortical_sloughing_outer_root_layer_peels_away_leaving_vascular_stele
- sudden_top_wilting_without_foliar_lesions
- dark_brown_to_black_discoloration_of_the_internal_root_tissue

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- upper_canopy_powdery_white_coatings
- raised_cinnamon_brown_leaf_pustules
- concentric_target_board_leaf_spots
- isolated_mid_stem_girdling_lesions_with_healthy_roots

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Fusarium Wilt (*Fusarium oxysporum*)
- Charcoal Rot (*Macrophomina phaseolina*)
- Sclerotinia Crown Rot (*Sclerotinia sclerotiorum*)
- Drought Stress / Herbicide Damage

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Root Rot shows physical disintegration, rot, and sloughing of root tissues below ground, whereas Fusarium Wilt primary symptoms show internal vascular discoloration with less initial cortical tissue rotting.
- Sclerotinia Crown Rot produces large black sclerotia and white cottony growth, whereas Pythium/Rhizoctonia Root Rot lacks large sclerotia and presents soft, water-soaked root decay.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Pythium/Rhizoctonia favor cool-to-moderate soil (15°C–22°C); Macrophomina favors hot, dry soils (above 30°C).

### Humidity

- High soil moisture / waterlogging favors Oomycete root rots; dry atmospheric conditions accelerate top wilt.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Poorly drained, saturated, or waterlogged soils during early root establishment promote pathogen germination and spread.

### Soil

- Compacted, heavy clay soils with poor drainage favor root rot complexes.

## Crop Stage

- Seedling emergence to early vegetative (V-2 to V-8) for damping-off/root rot; late vegetative to flowering for secondary root rot collapse.
