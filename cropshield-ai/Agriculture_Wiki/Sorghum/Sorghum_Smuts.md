## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Sorghum Grain Mold
- Sorghum Anthracnose
- Sorghum Ergot
- Sorghum Downy Mildew

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare the affected plant part before interpreting color.
- Determine whether the reproductive structure is replaced by a discrete smut sorus.
- Distinguish a dry powdery spore mass from superficial mold growth on the grain surface.
- Examine whether the symptom is localized to individual spikelets or reproductive structures rather than forming a continuous leaf lesion.
- Distinguish smut sori from normal grain discoloration and other panicle diseases.
- Treat uncertainty in identifying the panicle, spikelet, or grain structure as uncertainty rather than as a fact.
