## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Sorghum Leaf Blight
- Sorghum Anthracnose
- Sorghum Rust
- Nutrient Deficiency

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare the affected plant part before interpreting discoloration.
- Distinguish parallel chlorotic streaks from discrete necrotic lesions.
- Examine for downy growth when humidity is high and the tissue is recently affected.
- Compare the orientation and distribution of streaks across the leaf blade.
- Distinguish downy mildew symptoms from raised rust-colored pustules.
- Treat uncertainty in identifying surface growth or vein relationship as uncertainty, not as a fact.
