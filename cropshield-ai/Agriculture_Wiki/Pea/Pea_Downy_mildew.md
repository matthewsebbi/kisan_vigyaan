# Pea + Downy Mildew

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- stem
- pods
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- diffuse_discoloration
- localized_lesion
- surface_growth

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular`
- Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
- Width profile: `variable`
- Ends: `irregular`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

- lesions may range from small localized spots to larger coalescing areas on affected leaves

### Color

- Center / primary color: yellow_to_pale_green
- Margin: may be diffuse
- Surrounding tissue: green
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: pale_yellow, yellow, light_brown

### Texture

- Current texture: smooth_on_leaf_surface, may_become_dry_in_advanced_tissue
- Early appearance: `yellowish_leaf_areas_with_downy_growth_on_lower_surface`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `irregular`
- Distribution: `scattered_to_coalescing`
- Coalescence: `moderate_to_extensive`
- Vein relationship: `may_be_limited_by_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `leaf_blade`
- Surface association: `lower_leaf_surface`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- reduced_vigor
- premature_leaf_yellowing
- leaf_drop_in_severe_cases
- reduced_pod_development_in_severe_cases

## Hallmark Features

- pale_yellow_to_yellow_leaf_lesions
- downy_growth_on_lower_leaf_surface
- lesions_may_coalesce
- disease_is_favored_by_high_humidity_and_leaf_wetness

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- white_powdery_growth_covering_both_leaf_surfaces
- orange_to_rust_colored_pustules
- clearly_defined_circular_brown_lesions_without_downy_growth

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Pea Powdery Mildew
- Pea Rust
- Ascochyta Blight

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare the affected plant part before interpreting lesion color.
- Check the lower leaf surface for downy fungal growth.
- Distinguish downy growth from superficial powdery growth.
- Compare lesion geometry before treating an irregular discoloration as a discrete spot.
- Distinguish yellow leaf patches associated with downy growth from rust pustules.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Cool to moderate temperatures generally favor disease development.

### Humidity

- High relative humidity favors infection and sporulation.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Frequent moisture and prolonged leaf wetness favor disease development.
