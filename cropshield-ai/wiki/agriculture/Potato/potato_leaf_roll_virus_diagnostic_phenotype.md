# Potato Leaf Roll Virus (PLRV)

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- whole_plant
- tuber

### Symptom Class

- apical_leaf_rolling
- stunting
- phloem_necrosis

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `none_no_distinct_lesion`
- Elongation: `not_applicable`
- Width profile: `inward_upward_cupping`
- Ends: `not_applicable`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous`

### Size

- Affects whole leaves and canopy segments uniformly.

### Color

- Center / primary color: pale_green_to_chlorotic_yellow
- Margin: pinkish_red_to_purple_tint
- Surrounding tissue: yellow_chlorotic
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: pale_yellowish_green, purple, reddish

### Texture

- Current texture: stiff, thick, leathery, brittle, paper_rustle_sound
- Early appearance: `rolling_of_leaf_margins`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `upward_inward_rolling`
- Distribution: `systemic_whole_plant`
- Coalescence: `continuous`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `lower_leaves_in_secondary_infection_upper_leaves_in_primary`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- upright_stunted_growth_habit
- lower_leaves_roll_upward_and_inward
- net_necrosis_inside_tubers
- leathery_brittle_foliage

## Hallmark Features

- upward_and_inward_rolling_of_leaflets
- leathery_and_brittle_leaf_texture
- stunting_and_erect_plant_growth
- net_necrosis_internal_tuber_symptoms

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- water_soaked_blight_spots
- concentric_ring_target_spots
- black_crusty_sclerotia_on_tubers
- soft_rotting_stems

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Rhizoctonia aerial tuber symptom
- Blackleg top rolling
- Drought stress / Heat rolling

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Squeeze rolling leaf blade: PLRV leaves are stiff, thick, and break easily with a leathery rustle.
- Examine internal tuber slices for vascular network necrosis (net necrosis).
- Check for aphid vector activity and absence of stem-base cankers.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Symptoms express intensely in warm weather (20°C to 28°C); vector aphid population growth accelerates in warmth.

### Humidity

- Indirectly affected by humidity levels that support aphid populations.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Drought accentuates symptoms, but vector presence is key driver.

### Soil

- Nutrient deficient soils accentuate yellowing and purpling.

## Crop Stage

- Vegetative growth through tuber bulking and maturation.
