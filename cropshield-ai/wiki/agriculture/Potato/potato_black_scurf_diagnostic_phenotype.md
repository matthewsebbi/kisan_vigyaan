# Potato Black Scurf (Rhizoctonia Solani)

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- tuber
- sprout
- stem_base
- stolon

### Symptom Class

- superficial_sclerotia
- stem_canker
- aerial_tuber_formation

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_crusty_patches`
- Elongation: `variable`
- Width profile: `surface_crust`
- Ends: `not_applicable`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_clustered_crusts`

### Size

- Sclerotia on tubers range from <1 mm to over 10 mm irregularly raised specks.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown_to_pitch_black
- Margin: distinct_sharp
- Surrounding tissue: normal_skin
- Color transition: `abrupt`
- Current/overall colors: dark_black, reddish_brown_cankers

### Texture

- Current texture: hard, crusty, raised, soil_like_does_not_wash_off
- Early appearance: `brown_sunken_lesions_on_sprouts`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `random_superficial`
- Distribution: `scattered_on_tuber_surface`
- Coalescence: `clustered`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `tuber_skin_and_underground_stem_bases`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- delayed_or_uneven_emergence
- brown_sunken_cankers_on_underground_stems
- formation_of_green_aerial_tubers
- top_leaf_rolling_and_purpling

## Hallmark Features

- black_dirt_like_crusts_on_tubers_that_will_not_wash_off
- reddish_brown_sunken_cankers_on_stem_bases
- green_aerial_tubers_in_leaf_axils
- delayed_sprout_emergence

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- water_soaked_rapidly_expanding_foliar_blight
- concentric_rings_on_leaves
- yellow_mosaic_pattern_on_foliage
- soft_foul_smelling_tuber_rot

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Common Scab (*Streptomyces scabies*)
- Dirt / Soil Clods
- Charcoal Rot

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Attempt to wash off the black specks with water: Black Scurf sclerotia will not wash off, unlike soil particles.
- Distinguish raised black crusts from pitted or corky crater-like spots seen in Common Scab.
- Observe underground stem bases for brown necrotic sunken stem cankers.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Cool soils (10°C to 18°C) delay emergence and favor aggressiveness.

### Humidity

- Soil moisture: High soil moisture promotes spore activity and sclerotia development.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Wet spring planting conditions increase sprout damage.

### Soil

- Cold, poorly drained, heavy soils exacerbate stem infection.

## Crop Stage

- Emergence, sprouting, tuberization, and tuber maturation/harvest.
