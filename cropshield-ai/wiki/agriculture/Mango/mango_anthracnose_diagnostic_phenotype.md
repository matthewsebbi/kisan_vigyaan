# Mango Anthracnose

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- leaf_petiole
- inflorescence_panicle
- fruit_exocarp
- young_twigs

### Symptom Class

- discrete_lesion
- leaf_spot
- blossom_blight
- fruit_rot

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `angular_to_circular_spots_and_tear_stains`
- Elongation: `moderate_to_high_along_fruit_driplines`
- Width profile: `variable_spindle_to_irregular`
- Ends: `rounded_to_irregular`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_early_coalescing_late`

### Size

- Foliar spots: 1-5 mm in diameter; Fruit lesions: 2 mm to several centimeters; readily coalesces into large irregular dead zones.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown, black
- Margin: dark_brown, purple_black
- Surrounding tissue: chlorotic_yellow_halo
- Color transition: `abrupt`

### Texture

- Current texture: dry, brittle, depressed, sunken_on_fruit, shot_hole_cratering

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `random_on_leaves_vertical_tear_stains_on_fruit`

- Coalescence: `extensive_under_wet_conditions`
- Vein relationship: `constrained_by_veinlets_on_young_leaves`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `tender_flushing_leaves_open_inflorescences_and_maturing_fruit`

- angular_dark_brown_to_black_leaf_spots_with_shot_hole_centers

- star_shaped_raised_carky_cracks_on_fruit

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Mango Bacterial Black Spot (*Xanthomonas citri* pv. *mangiferaeindicae*)
- Mango Powdery Mildew (*Oidium mangiferae*)
- Pest injury / Thrips feeding scarring

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Lesion morphology: Anthracnose lesions on fruit are sunken and smooth/depressed with salmon pink spore masses in humidity, unlike Bacterial Black Spot which produces raised, star-cracked, black lesions with gummy exudate.
- Microscopic/Signs: Anthracnose exhibits dark acervuli and pink spore masses under moisture; Powdery Mildew produces white, powdery superficial mycelium.
- Leaf appearance: Anthracnose causes shot-holes on leaf blades; Bacterial Black Spot causes angular, raised, black water-soaked spots with yellow halos along veins.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 24°C to 30°C (Optimum range for conidial germination and infection)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (> 95% RH required for abundant spore production and infection)

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Free water or rain splashes essential for spore dispersal and germ tube penetration (prolonged leaf wetness > 12 hours)

### Soil

- Non-specific; indirect influence through tree vigor and canopy density

## Crop Stage

- Flushing stage (young leaves), flowering/anthesis (panicles), fruit set, and postharvest storage.
