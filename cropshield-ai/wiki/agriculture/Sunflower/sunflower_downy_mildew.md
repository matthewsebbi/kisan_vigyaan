# Sunflower Downy Mildew

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- stem
- whole_plant
- root_system

### Symptom Class

- systemic_chlorosis
- downy_sporulation
- plant_dwarfing

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `broad_chlorotic_bands_along_veins`
- Elongation: `high_along_main_veins`
- Width profile: `variable_expanding_outward_from_midrib`
- Ends: `diffuse`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_along_vascular_pathways`

### Size

- Can cover major portions of the leaf blade or entire leaf surfaces, combined with severe whole-plant stunting (plants reduced to 10–50 cm in height).

### Color

- Center / primary color: pale_green, creamy_yellow, bright_chlorotic_yellow
- Margin: pale_yellow_to_green
- Surrounding tissue: dark_green
- Color transition: `gradual_to_moderately_defined`
- Current/overall colors: pale_yellow_above, white_downy_below

### Texture

- Current texture: soft, velvety_downy_underside, leathery_thickened_leaf
- Early appearance: `pale_chlorotic_flecking_near_veins`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `vein_centered_longitudinal`
- Distribution: `systemic_symmetrical`
- Coalescence: `extensive_covering_entire_leaves`
- Vein relationship: `strongly_associated_with_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `systemic_from_base_to_top_of_plant`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- severe_stunting
- shortened_internodes
- horizontal_rigid_chlorotic_leaves
- erect_unbent_flowering_heads
- white_felt_like_growth_on_lower_leaf_surface

## Hallmark Features

- systemic_severe_plant_stunting_with_crowded_leaves
- broad_pale_yellow_chlorotic_zones_along_veins_on_upper_leaf_surface
- dense_white_velvety_downy_fungal_growth_on_lower_leaf_surface_opposite_chlorosis
- affected_heads_remain_upright_and_produce_empty_seeds

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- raised_powdery_rust_colored_pustules
- bleached_shredded_stem_base_with_black_sclerotia
- circular_necrotic_spots_with_concentric_rings
- severe_leaf_curling_with_mosaic_pattern

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Herbicide Injury (e.g., glyphosate or sulfonylurea drift)
- Sunflower Mosaic Virus
- Nitrogen Deficiency

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Presence of white downy sporulation exclusively on the lower leaf surface distinguishes Downy Mildew from herbicide injury or viral mosaic.
- Downy Mildew causes severe stunting and horizontal, brittle chlorotic leaves, unlike uniform yellowing from nitrogen deficiency.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Optimal temperature range: 12°C to 18°C (cool weather favors zoospore infection).

### Humidity

- Near 100% relative humidity required overnight for sporangia production.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Waterlogged, saturated soils or heavy rain early in the season are critical for oospore germination and primary root infection.

### Soil

- Poorly drained, heavy clay or compacted soils promote root-zone infection.

## Crop Stage

- Primary infection occurs at seedling emergence to early vegetative stage (V-2 to V-4); secondary foliar infection can occur later in cool, rainy weather.
