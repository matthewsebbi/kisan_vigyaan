# Sunflower Sclerotinia Stem Rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- stem
- basal_stem_crown
- root_system
- flower_head_capitulum

### Symptom Class

- water_soaked_lesion
- stem_rot_canker
- fungal_mycelial_growth
- sclerotial_formation

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `elongated_to_girdling_band`
- Elongation: `high_along_stem_axis`
- Width profile: `expanding_until_girdling`
- Ends: `irregular_blunt`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_expanding_lesion`

### Size

- Lesions often range from 5 cm to over 30 cm in length along the stem, expanding until fully girdling the stem or capitulum base.

### Color

- Center / primary color: tan, pale_brown, bleached_straw_white
- Margin: dark_brown, water_soaked_olive_green
- Surrounding tissue: pale_green_to_yellowing
- Color transition: `distinct_to_moderate`
- Current/overall colors: bleached_white, straw_tan, dark_brown, black_sclerotia

### Texture

- Current texture: soft, water_soaked, fibrous, shredded_stem_interior, hard_rigid_sclerotia
- Early appearance: `water_soaked_soft`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `longitudinal_and_circumferential`
- Distribution: `localized_expanding_to_systemic_wilt`
- Coalescence: `coalescing_around_stem_circumference`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `stem_base_soil_line_or_mid_stem_nodes`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- sudden_wilt
- lodging_stem_breakage
- shredded_stem_base
- white_cottony_mycelium
- black_sclerotia_in_pith
- head_rot_and_shattering

## Hallmark Features

- bleached_straw_colored_lesions
- dense_white_cottony_fungal_mycelium
- hard_black_irregular_sclerotia_inside_stem_pith
- shredded_fibrous_stem_breakage
- sudden_wilting_while_leaves_remain_attached

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- rusty_brown_powdery_pustules
- angular_leaf_spots_restricted_by_veins
- systemic_downy_white_growth_restricted_to_leaf_underside
- distinct_target_board_concentric_rings

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Phomopsis Stem Canker (*Diaporthe helianthi*)
- Verticillium Wilt (*Verticillium dahliae*)
- Charcoal Rot (*Macrophomina phaseolina*)

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Presence of hard, black sclerotia inside the hollowed or shredded pith confirms Sclerotinia over Phomopsis or Verticillium.
- Sclerotinia produces fluffy white mycelium under moist conditions, unlike Phomopsis or Charcoal Rot.
- Charcoal Rot exhibits tiny, dust-like black microsclerotia causing a gray-black stem discoloration, unlike the large black rat-dropping-like sclerotia of Sclerotinia.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Optimal temperature range: 15°C to 25°C (cool to moderate conditions).

### Humidity

- High relative humidity (> 80-85%) or prolonged periods of free moisture/dew.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Frequent rainfall, high water table, or overhead irrigation during flowering and stem elongation favors ascospore germination and infection.

### Soil

- Cool, moist soils promote sclerotial germination and apothecia development.

## Crop Stage

- Susceptible at all stages, but basal stem rot is most prominent during early vegetative to flowering stages, while head rot occurs from post-flowering to maturity.
