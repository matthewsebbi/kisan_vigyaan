# Finger Millet Leaf Spot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- leaf_sheath
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- discrete_foliar_spot
- cercospora_leaf_spot
- necrotic_lesion

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `oval_to_elongated`
- Elongation: `moderate_to_high`
- Width profile: `uniform_or_slightly_expanded`
- Ends: `blunt_rounded`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_scattered_to_coalescing`

### Size

- Lesions occur as numerous small spots or streak-like lesions on the leaf blade.
- Lesions may enlarge and coalesce under favorable disease conditions.
- Severe infection can cover substantial portions of the leaf.

### Color

- Center / primary color: `reddish_brown_to_brown`
- Margin: `dark_brown_to_dark_purple`
- Surrounding tissue: green
- Color transition: `sharp_to_gradual`
- Current/overall colors: reddish_brown, brown, dark_brown, dark_purple, grayish

### Texture

- Current texture: dry, necrotic, flat
- Early appearance: `small_brown_spot_or_streak`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `longitudinal_or_variable`
- Distribution: `discrete_scattered`
- Coalescence: `may_coalesce`
- Vein relationship: `crosses_or_ignores_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `leaf_blade`
- Secondary location: `leaf_sheath`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- multiple_leaf_spots
- progressive_leaf_necrosis
- reduced_green_leaf_area
- premature_leaf_drying_in_severe_cases
- severe_defoliation_or_blight_like_appearance_when_lesions_coalesce

## Hallmark Features

- numerous_reddish_brown_leaf_spots
- elongated_or_streak_like_lesions
- dark_brown_to_dark_purple_margins
- lesions_may_coalesce
- predominantly_foliar_symptoms
- cercospora_leaf_spot_is_distinct_from_spindle_shaped_blast_lesions

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- spindle_diamond_gray_centered_lesions
- blackened_shrunken_neck_as_primary_symptom
- brown_to_black_fingers_with_poor_seed_set
- raised_powdery_rust_pustules
- enlarged_smut_spore_balls
- green_ear_panicle_transformation

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Finger Millet Brown Spot
- Finger Millet Blast
- Finger Millet Rust
- Banded Blight

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare lesion geometry before interpreting brown coloration.
- Distinguish numerous small reddish-brown spots and streaks from the larger spindle-shaped lesions of blast.
- Distinguish flat necrotic spots from raised rust pustules.
- Check whether symptoms are centered on leaves rather than the neck or fingers.
- Use center-to-margin color relationships as supporting evidence rather than relying on the word brown alone.
- Treat uncertainty in distinguishing Cercospora leaf spot from other small-millet leaf spots as uncertainty when image resolution is insufficient.
