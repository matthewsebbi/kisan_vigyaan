# Cashew Leaf Blight

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- leaf_margin
- leaf_tip
- petiole
- tender_shoot_tips

### Symptom Class

- rapid_tissue_necrosis
- extensive_foliar_blight
- curling_and_wither

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `large_irregular_expanding_blotch`
- Elongation: `high_along_leaf_margins_and_veins`
- Width profile: `variable_expanding_broadly`
- Ends: `diffuse_wavy`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_coalescent_blight`

### Size

- Blight patches rapidly expand from 20 mm to covering over 50%–100% of the entire leaf surface area.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_reddish_brown_to_burnt_umber
- Margin: water_soaked_dark_olive_or_yellow_chlorotic_halo
- Surrounding tissue: chlorotic_yellow_fading_to_green
- Color transition: `gradual_to_diffuse`
- Current/overall colors: dark_brown, scorched_black, pale_tan, chlorotic_yellow

### Texture

- Current texture: brittle, dry, papery, scorched, crumpled
- Early appearance: `soft_water_soaked_dull_dark_green_patch`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `basipetal_from_tip_or_inward_from_margins`
- Distribution: `continuous_blight_spreading_across_canopy`
- Coalescence: `extensive_entire_leaf_affected`
- Vein relationship: `crosses_veins_unrestricted`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `leaf_tips_leaf_margins_and_apical_flushes`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- scorched_canopy_appearance
- extensive_premature_defoliation
- withered_crinkled_apical_shoots
- reduction_in_photosynthetic_area
- field_level_fire_scorched_visual_effect

## Hallmark Features

- rapid_expanding_dark_brown_necrotic_blighting_of_tender_foliage
- leaf_edges_curl_inward_and_shrivel_resembling_fire_scorch
- lack_of_strictly_confined_spot_margins
- rapid_spread_during_monsoon/flush_seasons_leading_to_defoliation

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present:

- tiny_isolated_pinhead_spots_that_do_not_coalesce
- clear_amber_resin_bleeding_from_main_trunk
- white_powdery_coating_on_underside_of_leaf
- healthy_green_foliage_with_only_flower_panicle_rot

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Cashew Anthracnose leaf phase
- Phytophthora foliar blight
- Wind burn / salt spray scorch
- Chemical / fertilizer burn

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Leaf Blight (*Cylindrocladium* / *Phomopsis* / *Xanthomonas* spp.) rapidly engulfs the leaf margins and tips causing total foliar shriveling, whereas Leaf Spot remains strictly localized.
- Chemical/wind burn exhibits uniform marginal browning across the entire outer canopy edge without pathogen sporulation or water-soaked wet margins.
- Anthracnose blight often produces distinctive pinkish/salmon spore masses in humid conditions, which may be absent in bacterial or *Cylindrocladium* leaf blights.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 25°C – 32°C.

### Humidity

- Extremely high humidity (> 90%) and saturated air.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Heavy, continuous rainfall, driving winds, and persistent leaf wetness trigger rapid epidemic spread.

### Soil

- Poor soil drainage, high water table, and poorly ventilated heavy canopy microclimates.

## Crop Stage

- Foliar flush stage, shoot emergence, young vegetative growth.
