# Soybean Bacterial Pustule

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- petiole
- stem

### Symptom Class

- bacterial_pustule
- necrotic_spot
- foliar_chlorosis

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `small_specks_to_angular_irregular_spots`
- Elongation: `low`
- Width profile: `variable`
- Ends: `variable`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete_becoming_coalescent`

### Size

- Individual spots measure 1 mm to 3 mm, coalescing into larger ragged necrotic areas that tear easily under wind.

### Color

- Center / primary color: pale_yellow_to_reddish_brown
- Margin: dark_brown
- Surrounding tissue: bright_yellow_chlorotic_halo
- Color transition: `abrupt`
- Current/overall colors: yellowish_green, reddish_brown, tan_necrotic_centers

### Texture

- Current texture: raised_central_pustule, dry, brittle, ragged
- Early appearance: `pale_green_specks_with_raised_centers`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `random`
- Distribution: `scattered_across_leaf_blade`
- Coalescence: `coalescing_into_large_blighted_patches`
- Vein relationship: `partially_restricted_by_small_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `upper_and_middle_canopy_leaves`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- ragged_tattered_leaf_appearance
- premature_leaf_drop_in_severe_cases
- yellowing_canopy_aspect
- reduced_photosynthetic_area

## Hallmark Features

- tiny_raised_light_colored_pustules_in_centers_of_lesions
- pustules_most_prominent_on_lower_abaxial_leaf_surface
- bright_yellow_chlorotic_halo_surrounding_reddish_brown_spots
- absence_of_spore_masses_or_powder_when_pustules_are_disturbed

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- powdery_spores_rubbing_off_pustules
- water_soaked_translucent_margins_with_bacterial_ooze_droplets (indicates Bacterial Blight)
- white_cottony_mycelium_on_stems
- charcoal_black_microsclerotia_in_pith

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Asian Soybean Rust (*Phakopsora pachyrhizi*)
- Soybean Bacterial Blight (*Pseudomonas savastanoi pv. glycinea*)
- Brown Spot (*Septoria glycines*)

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Bacterial Pustule lesions feature a non-powdery raised bump usually without water-soaked margins, whereas Asian Soybean Rust has raised volcano-like pustules that release pale powdery spores.
- Bacterial Blight shows water-soaked margins around lesions, especially in cool/wet weather, whereas Bacterial Pustule forms distinct central pustular crests in warm weather.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Warm to hot weather (30°C to 35°C) favors disease development.

### Humidity

- High relative humidity or splashing water needed for entry through stomata and wounds.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Frequent rainstorms with wind facilitate bacterial dispersal and injury-assisted infection.

### Soil

- Normal crop nutrition; high nitrogen can increase foliage susceptibility.

## Crop Stage

- Can infect at any growth stage, but symptoms become most prominent during mid-to-late vegetative and reproductive stages.
