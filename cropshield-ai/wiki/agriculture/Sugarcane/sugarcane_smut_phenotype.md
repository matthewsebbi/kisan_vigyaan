# Sugarcane Smut

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- growing_point
- stalk_top
- leaf_sheath

### Symptom Class

- sorus_formation
- whip_like_structure
- shoot_deformation

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `curved_or_straight_elongated_whip`
- Elongation: `extremely_high`
- Width profile: `cylindrical_tapering`
- Ends: `pointed`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_singular_structure`

### Size

- Whip length ranges from a few centimeters up to 1.5 meters

### Color

- Center / primary color: silvery_gray_membrane_turning_coal_black
- Margin: not_applicable
- Surrounding tissue: green
- Color transition: `sharp`
- Current/overall colors: silvery_white, gray, black

### Texture

- Current texture: dusty, powdery_spore_mass, fibrous_core
- Early appearance: `smooth_silvery_thin_membrane`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `vertical_upward_growth`
- Distribution: `terminal_apical_shoot`
- Coalescence: `not_applicable`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `terminal_meristem_growing_point`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- whip_like_structure_arising_from_shoot_apex
- slender_stunted_canes
- thin_tillers_with_narrow_small_leaves
- profuse_tillering_grass_like_habit_in_severe_cases
- severe_yield_loss

## Hallmark Features

- unbranched_whip_like_sooty_black_structure_from_terminal_shoot
- early_stage_silvery_gray_membrane_rupturing_to_release_black_spores
- thin_slender_stalks_with_short_internodes

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- internal_red_stalk_with_white_spots
- distorted_leaf_top_without_black_spore_whip
- orange_rust_pustules_on_leaves
- ring_spot_lesions

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Pokkah Boeng (Top rot phase)
- Grassy Shoot Disease (when smut causes excessive thin tillering without whip formation)

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- The presence of the black, powdery, whip-like sorus is pathognomonic for Sugarcane Smut.
- Unlike Pokkah Boeng, Smut does not cause ladder-like cut marks or leaf distortion without spore structures.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 25°C - 35°C (Warm and dry weather favors spore release and germination)

### Humidity

- Low to moderate relative humidity promotes airborne dispersal of teliospores.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Dry conditions facilitate spore wind dispersal; light rain or dew aids infection during bud germination.

### Soil

- Drought-stressed soils increase crop susceptibility.

## Crop Stage

- Early tillering to early cane elongation phase (also common in ratoon crops)
