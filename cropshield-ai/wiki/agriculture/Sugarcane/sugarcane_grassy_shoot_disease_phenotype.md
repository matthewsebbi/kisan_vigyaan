# Sugarcane Grassy Shoot Disease

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- whole_plant
- tillers
- leaf_blade

### Symptom Class

- excessive_tillering
- chlorosis
- stunting

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `none_no_distinct_lesion`
- Elongation: `high_at_leaf_level`
- Width profile: `continuous`
- Ends: `not_applicable`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous`

### Size

- Affects whole stool; leaves are narrow, short, and thin

### Color

- Center / primary color: not_applicable_no_distinct_lesion
- Margin: not_applicable
- Surrounding tissue: pale_green
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: albino_white, pale_yellow, yellowish_green

### Texture

- Current texture: soft, papery, thin, weak
- Early appearance: `chlorotic_striations`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `longitudinal`
- Distribution: `generalized_systemic_foliar_chlorosis`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `parallel_to_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `base_of_stool_and_emerging_tillers`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- proliferation_of_numerous_thin_slender_tillers
- dense_grassy_bushy_appearance_of_stool
- total_absence_of_cane_formation_in_severe_cases
- albino_white_or_yellowish_leaves_on_tillers
- premature_and_excessive_bud_sprouting

## Hallmark Features

- stool_transformed_into_a_dense_bushy_grassy_cluster
- fine_narrow_albino_white_to_pale_yellow_leaves
- failure_of_tillers_to_develop_into_normal_thick_canes

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- dark_red_internal_stalk_discoloration
- whip_like_black_spore_structures
- discrete_necrotic_leaf_spots
- red_stripes_with_bacterial_exudate

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Sugarcane White Leaf Disease (Phytoplasma)
- Iron Chlorosis / Micronutrient Deficiency
- Ratoon Stunting Disease (causes stunting but not excessive albino tillering)

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Grassy Shoot Disease produces mass proliferation of thin, grassy tillers, unlike simple micronutrient chlorosis.
- Total albino tillers are common in GSD, whereas iron chlorosis shows interveinal chlorosis across leaves without massive bushy tillering.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 28°C - 34°C (Warm tropical conditions favor insect vector population)

### Humidity

- High relative humidity supporting aphid and leafhopper vectors.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Irrigated or humid environments with dense vector populations.

### Soil

- Nutrient-depleted soils exacerbate stunting symptoms.

## Crop Stage

- Germination stage, tillering stage, and ratoon crop initiation
