# Cotton Vascular Wilt

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- vascular_system
- leaf_blade
- main_stem
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- vascular_discoloration
- foliar_chlorosis_and_necrosis
- wilting_and_epinasty

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `interveinal_marginal_chlorotic_yellowing_and_browning`
- Elongation: `high_along_leaf_margins_and_vascular_tracts`
- Width profile: `variable`
- Ends: `tapered`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_along_veins`

### Size

- Spreads systemically from lower leaves to entire canopy; vascular browning extends throughout stem and root core.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown_to_blackish_ring_in_dissected_vascular_ring
- Margin: yellow_to_reddish_brown
- Surrounding tissue: chlorotic_yellow_to_dull_green
- Color transition: `gradual_to_distinct`
- Current/overall colors: yellow, reddish_brown, dark_brown, black

### Texture

- Current texture: dry, brittle, withered, papery_leaves
- Early appearance: `drooping_vein_clearing_and_marginal_chlorosis`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `interveinal_and_vascular_bound`
- Distribution: `acropetal_from_lower_to_upper_leaves`
- Coalescence: `extensive`
- Vein relationship: `vein_clearing_and_interveinal_necrosis`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `lower_leaves_first_progressing_upward_and_internal_stem_xylem`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- unilateral_leaf_yellowing_and_wilting_tiger_stripe_pattern
- dark_brown_to_blackish_vascular_ring_visible_upon_stem_splitting
- leaf_drop_leaving_bare_stalks_top_wilting
- severe_stunting_and_plant_death
- patchy_field_distribution_wilt_sick_patches

## Hallmark Features

- continuous_dark_brown_or_black_discoloration_of_internal_vascular_xylem_ring
- interveinal_yellowing_and_browning_leaf_symptom
- acropetal_wilting_and_defoliation_from_bottom_to_top

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- water_soaked_angular_leaf_spots
- salmon_pink_slimy_spore_masses_on_bolls
- external_bark_shredding_and_yellow_fungal_mats_at_soil_line
- foliar_enations_and_leaf_curling

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Fusarium Wilt vs Verticillium Wilt
- Cotton Root Rot
- Drought / Waterlogging stress

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Split stem reveals dark brown/black vascular discoloration ring in vascular wilt, unlike root rots where bark shredded externally.
- Verticillium wilt typically occurs in cooler temperatures with mottling, whereas Fusarium wilt prefers warm acidic/sandy soils and causes dark vascular staining.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- Fusarium Wilt: 28°C - 32°C (Warm)
- Verticillium Wilt: 20°C - 25°C (Cooler)

### Humidity

- Moderate to high relative humidity.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Wet soil followed by dry periods enhances wilt development and expression.

### Soil

- Sandy, acidic soils associated with root-knot nematodes favor Fusarium wilt; heavy alkaline soils favor Verticillium.

## Crop Stage

- Flowering to boll formation stage (can manifest from seedling to maturity)
