# Lentil Powdery Mildew

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   petiole
-   stem
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   superficial_fungal_growth
-   diffuse_discoloration
-   chlorotic_patches

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `irregular_superficial_patches`
-   Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
-   Width profile: `variable`
-   Ends: `irregular`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small white powdery colonies that enlarge and may merge into larger
    irregular patches covering portions of the leaf surface

### Color

-   Center / primary color: white, gray_white
-   Margin: pale_green, yellow_green
-   Surrounding tissue: green
-   Color transition: `gradual`
-   Current/overall colors: white, gray_white, pale_green, yellow_green,
    green

### Texture

-   Current texture: powdery, superficial, slightly_rough
-   Early appearance: `small_white_powdery_patches`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: `irregular`
-   Distribution: `discrete_patches_that_may_coalesce`
-   Coalescence: `moderate_to_extensive`
-   Vein relationship: `crosses_or_ignores_veins`

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location: `leaf_surface`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   white_powdery_leaf_growth
-   expanding_superficial_patches
-   leaf_chlorosis
-   reduced_green_leaf_area
-   premature_leaf_drying_in_severe_cases
-   multiple_leaves_affected

## Hallmark Features

-   white_to_gray_white_powdery_surface_growth
-   superficial_fungal_colonies
-   irregular_expanding_patches
-   patch_coalescence
-   contrasting_green_and_white_leaf_surface

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   orange_brown_raised_pustules
-   diffuse_whole_plant_wilting
-   discrete_circular_necrotic_spots
-   soft_root_rot
-   bright_yellow_green_mosaic_pattern

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Lentil Rust
-   Lentil Wilt
-   Other Fungal Leaf Spots
-   Nutrient Deficiency

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare superficial white powdery growth with raised orange rust
    pustules.
-   Distinguish powdery colonies from discrete necrotic leaf spots.
-   Compare surface texture before interpreting color alone.
-   Distinguish foliar powdery growth from whole-plant vascular wilt.
-   Treat uncertainty in surface growth identification as uncertainty,
    not as a fact.
