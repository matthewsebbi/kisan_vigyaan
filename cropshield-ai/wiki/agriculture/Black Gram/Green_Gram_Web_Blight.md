# Green Gram Web Blight

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

-   leaf_blade
-   petiole
-   stem
-   whole_plant

### Symptom Class

-   necrotic_lesions
-   web_like_fungal_growth
-   leaf_blight

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

-   Primary shape: `irregular_circular_to_angular_lesions`
-   Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
-   Width profile: `variable`
-   Ends: `irregular`
-   Discrete or continuous: `discrete_to_coalescing`

### Size

-   small irregular leaf lesions may enlarge rapidly and merge,
    producing extensive necrotic areas across the leaf surface

### Color

-   Center / primary color: brown, reddish_brown, gray_brown
-   Margin: dark_brown, reddish_brown
-   Surrounding tissue: green
-   Color transition: `sharp_to_gradual`
-   Current/overall colors: brown, reddish_brown, gray_brown,
    dark_brown, yellow_brown

### Texture

-   Current texture: dry_papery, rough, web_like,
    brittle_in_severe_stage
-   Early appearance: `small_water_soaked_or_pale_brown_spots`

### Pattern and Distribution

-   Orientation: irregular
-   Distribution: irregular_scattered_to_coalescing
-   Coalescence: extensive
-   Vein relationship: crosses_or_ignores_veins

### Spatial Location

-   Primary location: leaf_blade

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

-   rapidly_expanding_leaf_necrosis
-   lesions_merge_into_large_blighted_areas
-   web_like_fungal_growth_between_or_over_affected_tissues
-   extensive_leaf_drying
-   reduced_green_leaf_area

## Hallmark Features

-   irregular_brown_to_reddish_brown_leaf_lesions
-   rapid_lesion_expansion
-   extensive_coalescence
-   web_like_fungal_growth
-   severe_leaf_blight_and_drying

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   bright_yellow_and_green_mosaic_without_necrotic_lesions
-   discrete_small_leaf_spots_without_coalescence
-   white_powdery_surface_growth
-   raised_orange_brown_pustules
-   water_soaked_angular_bacterial_lesions_without_web_like_growth

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Green Gram Cercospora Leaf Spot
-   Green Gram Anthracnose
-   Green Gram Powdery Mildew
-   Green Gram Bacterial Leaf Blight

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare lesion expansion and coalescence before interpreting
    individual lesion color.
-   Distinguish extensive irregular blight from discrete leaf spots.
-   Check for web-like fungal growth on or between affected tissues.
-   Compare surface texture with powdery mildew and raised rust-like
    structures.
-   Treat uncertainty in web-like growth or lesion boundaries as
    uncertainty, not as a fact.
