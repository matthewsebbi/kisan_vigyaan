## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   narrow_yellow_pustule_rows_on_leaves
-   scattered_orange_brown_leaf_pustules
-   elongated_black_pustules_on_stems
-   grain_with_only_partial_dark_internal_discoloration

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Wheat Karnal Bunt
-   Wheat Stripe / Yellow Rust
-   Wheat Leaf / Brown Rust
-   Wheat Stem / Black Rust

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare the affected plant part before interpreting dark coloration.
-   Distinguish whole-spike or spikelet replacement from symptoms
    confined to individual grains.
-   Compare the external powdery spore mass with internal or partially
    exposed grain discoloration.
-   Distinguish reproductive smut structures from leaf or stem rust
    pustules.
-   Treat uncertainty in spikelet replacement or spore-mass
    identification as uncertainty rather than as a fact.
