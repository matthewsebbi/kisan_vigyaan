## Hallmark Features

-   progressive_wilting_without_initial_distinct_leaf_lesions
-   yellowing_and_drooping_leaflets
-   vascular_browning
-   progression_from_affected_branches_to_whole_plant
-   severe_plant_decline_and_death

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are
clearly present.

-   extensive_surface_root_lesions_with_black_pycnidia
-   soft_water_soaked_root_and_collar_rot
-   white_powdery_leaf_growth
-   discrete_circular_leaf_spots
-   bright_yellow_green_mosaic_pattern

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

-   Chickpea Dry Root Rot
-   Chickpea Wet Root Rot
-   Collar Rot
-   Other Vascular Wilts

### Diagnostic Discriminators

-   Compare vascular discoloration and progressive wilt with external
    root decay.
-   Inspect roots and lower stem before interpreting leaf yellowing as a
    foliar disease.
-   Distinguish vascular browning from dark external root lesions.
-   Compare dry versus soft or water-soaked root and collar symptoms.
-   Treat uncertainty in vascular discoloration or root condition as
    uncertainty, not as a fact.
