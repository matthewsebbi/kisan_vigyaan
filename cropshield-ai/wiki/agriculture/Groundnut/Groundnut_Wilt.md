## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Groundnut Stem rot
- Groundnut Collar rot
- Groundnut Root rot
- Groundnut Late leaf spot
- Groundnut Early leaf spot

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare the affected plant part before interpreting leaf color.
- Distinguish whole-plant wilting from diseases whose primary feature is a discrete leaf lesion.
- Examine the lower stem, collar, and roots when possible rather than relying only on foliar symptoms.
- Compare vascular discoloration with external stem or collar lesions.
- Treat field-level patchiness as supportive context rather than as a diagnosis by itself.
- Treat uncertainty in root, stem, or collar examination as uncertainty rather than as a confirmed internal symptom.
