# Sorghum Anthracnose

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- leaf_blade
- leaf_sheath
- panicle
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- discrete_lesions
- necrotic_spots

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `oval_circular`
- Elongation: `low_to_moderate`
- Width profile: `wider_at_center`
- Ends: `blunt_rounded`
- Discrete or continuous: `discrete`

### Size

- small to medium lesions, typically a few millimeters across; individual lesions may enlarge and coalesce under severe disease conditions

### Color

- Center / primary color: tan, straw_colored, gray
- Margin: reddish_brown, purple_red, dark_brown
- Surrounding tissue: green
- Color transition: `sharp_two_tone`
- Current/overall colors: tan, straw_colored, gray, reddish_brown, purple_red, dark_brown

### Texture

- Current texture: dry_papery, slightly_rough
- Early appearance: `unclear`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `irregular`
- Distribution: `discrete_coalescing`
- Coalescence: `moderate_to_extensive`
- Vein relationship: `crosses_or_ignores_veins`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `leaf_blade`
- lesions may also occur on leaf sheaths and panicles

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- numerous reddish_brown_to_purple leaf spots
- tan_to_straw_colored lesion centers
- dark reddish_brown lesion margins
- lesions may enlarge and merge
- severe infection may cause extensive leaf necrosis
- panicle tissues may develop discoloration and necrosis
- premature drying of severely affected foliage

## Hallmark Features

- discrete_oval_to_circular_lesions
- reddish_brown_to_purple_margins
- tan_to_straw_colored_centers
- contrasting_center_and_margin_colors
- lesions_may_coalesce_under_severe_conditions

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- continuous_uniform_yellow_discoloration_without_distinct_lesions
- spindle_diamond_lesions
- long_linear_streaks
- raised_rust_colored_pustules
- grain_smut_balls

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Sorghum Leaf Blight
- Sorghum Gray Leaf Spot
- Sorghum Rust
- Sorghum Target Leaf Spot

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare the affected plant part before interpreting lesion color.
- Compare lesion geometry before treating an elongated symptom as a streak.
- Compare lesion center and margin colors separately.
- Distinguish discrete lesions from continuous or coalescing blight.
- Examine mature lesions for small dark fruiting structures when visible.
- Distinguish anthracnose lesions from raised rust-colored pustules.
- Distinguish anthracnose from other leaf spots using lesion shape, margin color, center color, and distribution.
- Treat uncertainty in plant-part identification or lesion boundaries as uncertainty, not as a fact.
