## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Niger Seed + Alternaria leaf spot
- Niger Seed + Powdery mildew
- Niger Seed + Rust

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Compare the affected plant part before interpreting lesion color.
- Gray centers with straw-to-brown spot coloration are useful discriminators when clearly visible.
- Distinguish Cercospora-type discrete spots from Alternaria lesions with conspicuous concentric rings.
- Compare individual lesion geometry before interpreting extensive defoliation.
- Treat coalescence as a severity feature rather than as the defining shape of an individual lesion.
- Treat uncertainty in lesion center color as uncertainty rather than assuming gray tissue from image compression or lighting.
