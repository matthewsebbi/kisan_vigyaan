# Niger Seed + Root rot

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- roots
- lower_stem
- whole_plant

### Symptom Class

- root_rot
- basal_rot
- wilt_and_decline

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `irregular_root_rot_lesion`
- Elongation: `variable_along_root`
- Width profile: `localized_to_root_tissue`
- Ends: `irregular`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_or_coalescing_on_affected_roots`

### Size

- affected roots may show discoloration over substantial portions of the root system
- severity can progress from root infection to basal stem blackening and whole-plant decline

### Color

- Center / primary color: `light_blackish_to_black`
- Margin: `dark_brown_to_black`
- Surrounding tissue: healthy root tissue is lighter; above-ground foliage may remain green initially and later become yellow or wilted
- Color transition: `gradual`
- Current/overall colors: light_blackish, black, dark_brown, yellow, wilted_brown

### Texture

- Current texture: brittle_roots, weakened_root_tissue, dry_in_advanced_rot
- Early appearance: `root_discoloration`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `along_root_and_basal_stem`
- Distribution: `root_system_with_possible_upward_stem_extension`
- Coalescence: `extensive_in_severe_cases`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `root_system`
- Secondary locations: lower_stem, soil_line, whole_plant

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- wilting
- yellowing
- reduced_vigor
- root_system_decline

- roots_become_light_blackish_to_black

- affected_roots_are_brittle
- blackening_can_extend_from_ground_level_upward_on_stem

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present.

- primary_leaf_spots_without_root_or_basal_symptoms
- white_powdery_growth_on_leaf_surface
- rust_pustules
- collar_only_lesion_without_root_involvement
- diffuse_wilt_without_any_root_or_stem_evidence

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Niger Seed + Collar rot
- Niger Seed + Wilt
- Niger Seed + Stem/root rot
- Niger Seed + Damping-off

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Examine roots before interpreting whole-plant yellowing or wilting.
- Blackish-to-black roots with black sclerotia and brittleness are the documented Niger stem/root-rot phenotype.
- Distinguish root-originating blackening from a lesion confined to the collar.
- Check whether blackening extends upward from ground level onto the stem.
- Treat absence of visible sclerotia cautiously because they may not be visible in every image or stage.
- The ICAR-IIOR Niger guide identifies this disease as stem/root rot caused by *Macrophomina phaseolina*. citeturn0search5
