# Citrus Gummosis (*Phytophthora citrophthora* / *Phytophthora nicotianae*)

## Diagnostic Phenotype

### Primary Affected Plant Parts

- trunk_bark
- crown_roots
- main_branches

### Symptom Class

- exudative_bark_canker

### Lesion / Symptom Geometry

- Primary shape: `vertical_cracks_or_longitudinal_cankers`
- Elongation: `high_along_trunk`
- Width profile: `variable`
- Ends: `tapered`
- Discrete or continuous: `continuous_patch`

### Size

- Variable; can span several centimeters to girdling the main trunk entirely.

### Color

- Center / primary color: dark_brown_to_black_canker
- Margin: reddish_brown_necrotic_edge
- Surrounding tissue: darkened_moist_bark
- Color transition: `gradual_to_distinct_under_bark`
- Current/overall colors: amber_gum, dark_brown, reddish_brown

### Texture

- Current texture: sticky_gum_exudate, cracked_bark, hardened_resinous_droplets
- Early appearance: `water_soaked_dark_bark`

### Pattern and Distribution

- Orientation: `longitudinal`
- Distribution: `localized_trunk_base`
- Coalescence: `extensive_under_bark`
- Vein relationship: `not_applicable`

### Spatial Location

- Primary location: `lower_trunk_scion_union_and_main_scaffolds`

### Whole-Plant / Field-Level Features

- water_soaked_dark_bark
- gum_exudation_from_cracks
- canopy_yellowing_and_thinning
- twig_dieback
- tree_decline_or_collapse

## Hallmark Features

- profuse_amber_coloured_gum_exudation
- longitudinal_bark_cracking
- reddish_brown_discoloration_of_cambium_under_bark
- localized_at_trunk_base_or_graft_union

## Negative / Exclusion Features

These features should reduce confidence in this diagnosis when they are clearly present:

- raised_corky_leaf_spots
- powdery_white_spores
- leaf_miner_tunnels
- asymmetrical_yellow_foliar_mottle

## Differential Diagnosis

### Similar Problems

- Psorosis (Citrus Psorosis Virus)
- Rio Grande Gummosis
- Foot Rot (*Phytophthora* spp.)

### Diagnostic Discriminators

- Psorosis is characterized by scaling and flaking of trunk bark rather than deep resinous gumming from wood cracks.
- Rio Grande Gummosis is associated with heart rot and open wood lesions rather than cambial decay starting from soil level.

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature

- 20°C – 28°C optimal for infection.

### Humidity

- High relative humidity promotes spore germination and sporangia production.

### Rainfall / Moisture

- Waterlogged soil, standing water, or heavy rain splashing favors pathogen movement.

### Soil

- Heavy, poorly drained, clay soils significantly increase disease incidence.

## Crop Stage

- Mature bearing trees, particularly those on susceptible rootstocks, under saturated soil conditions.
